# Small systems

Part of CLAUDE.md, moved out to keep every session's context small. Read it before working in this area; keep it current.

  - Colors in panels and Settings are `widgets/KisStudioColorField`: a swatch whose click opens the color panel's picker (`KisStudioColorWheel`) with the color written out (any format `KisStudioColorWheel::colorOfText` reads), the paint colors, an eyedropper and, where it may be see-through, Opacity; right-click resets. Never Krita's color dialog. The eyedropper (`KisStudioColorDropper`, a `qApp` filter) judges the canvas by the widget an event goes to, not `QApplication::widgetAt` (which asks Windows and finds another program's window whenever one is in front), reads only inside the image, and takes what the owner's sampler gives (effects: the layer as it was, not the preview).
  - Studio popups (`widgets/KisStudioPopup`): a glass panel in its own window, closed by a press elsewhere unless pinned; popups opened from it count as inside (`KisPopupCloser`). A plain `QWidget` (exactly that class, by `typeid`, without mouse tracking) counts as background that drags the window (`KisStudioGlass::setMovableByBackground`): give interactive widgets their own class. Checking the meta object counted classes without `Q_OBJECT` as plain, so the color picker and swatches in a color field's popup dragged the window on a click and picked only on a double click. Popups and dragged dialogs stay on screen (`DialogMover::onScreen`).
  - Toggling: a press on a popup's anchor closes it and is swallowed, so buttons toggle their popups (`KisPopupCloser::installToggle`, for every popup and menu; `popupBeside` passes the anchor). A press within 150 ms after one closed it, and Qt's double click, are swallowed too. Only closing by the anchor arms this (arming on every close swallowed fast clicks between buttons); input-less windows (a floating message) aren't popups. Buttons need no guards of their own. The owner finds a second click that reopens a popup obviously wrong.
  - An icon-only button whose press opens its menu (⋯) has no menu arrow (`menuArrowLeftOut`).
  - Empty lists say why they're empty (`KisStudioEmptyStates`).
  - Messages over the picture are for failures, warnings and live values while dragging. A change shows where it happened (`KisStudioFlash`: the brush chip lights for a brush picked by a key, the top bar's size or opacity for keys that change them), over the picture only when that control is tucked away.
  - Layer rows (`plugins/dockers/skissa_layers`: `LayerBox`, `NodeDelegate`): a sideways drag is the opacity (no slider); blend mode and opacity show only when unusual, on hover for the current row, always in touch mode (`updateRowShown`); double-click types, right-click makes it full (`opacityRect`). Let go at 0%, the layer is hidden instead, keeping its opacity, as one undo step. A thumbnail held still (`holdInterval`) shows its layer alone until let go, however the pointer drifts meanwhile (Krita's isolate mode, `NodeView` `soloTimer`; a move first is a drag); Ctrl+click on it selects its pixels (Krita's); a double-click on it opens the layer's properties, which open beside the pointer with nothing typed in yet, and pointing back at the canvas gives it the keys again (`CanvasSwitcher::takeKeysFromToolWindow`, for any tool window whose focus isn't typing), so Space pans while they are open. A filter mask's properties are its effect's own panel in a pinned Studio popup (`KisStudioMaskEditor`, from `KisMaskManager::maskProperties`), the mask updated live, ✓ one step, ✕ or Escape back: Krita's modal dialog held the canvas and listed every old filter. A press on the row's blend mode is passed to the row, so a sideways drag there is the opacity; only a click opens the modes. A row's switches (lock, clip, alpha…) flip when let go where pressed, so a sideways drag from one is the opacity too. In tall rows the switches and the eye sit in the row's middle and the second line ends before the switches; the mode box's margins go outside that line (its text is the line's height). Ctrl+Shift+X crops the image to the selection, or without one to the current layer's paint (`resizeimagetolayer`, `KisLayerManager::imageResizeToActiveLayer`). A tag being renamed isn't drawn under its field (`KisTagItemDelegate::renaming`), as a layer's name isn't. The blend mode and opacity on a row are in the quiet ink (`secondaryInk`), not see-through ink, which read faint on light themes. A filter layer's properties open in the same Studio panel as a filter mask's (`KisStudioMaskEditor`).
  - Shortcuts have places (`KisStudioShortcuts::toolsOf`/`shareContext`): tool actions work only while their tools are in use; only keys in the same place clash, and the Keys page (`dialogs/KisKeysPage`, saving 300 ms after an edit, `storeEdited`) marks only those. A key recorded anywhere (card, menu, Find, Keys page) is taken from the other command in its place, with a note naming it: two commands on one key in one place run neither. Lists of actions joined from several places must be made unique, by name too.
  - The color panel's picker (`ColorPicker` in `KisStudioColorPanel.cpp`) is two choices in its style menu: hues around a Ring (clockwise from red at the top) or down a Strip beside the shape (`StudioColorHues`), and the shape: HSV's Triangle (Krita's upright one) or Square, or OKLCH (`StudioColorShape`). Sliders, a strip across and a diamond were tried in a demo and left out, as were other hue spacings (OKLCH, vivid OKLCH, a painter's RYB wheel): HSV spacing it is. In the HSV shapes, turning the hue keeps the color's OKLCH lightness (`StudioColorKeepLightness`, on by default, `holdLightness`): at the saturation last picked, or with less where full value is too dark, which comes back with the hue. OKLCH lightness, not luminance, so it matches the value view. The OKLCH shape keeps lightness by itself. `KisStudioColorWheel` is that picker on its own, in the style chosen there, inside `KisStudioColorField`'s popup.
  - The OKLCH picker's ring and the hue slider are spaced as an HSV wheel (`oklchHueOfHsv`/`hsvHueOfOklch`, one monotonic table `ringHues` in `KisStudioColorPanel.cpp`); the owner prefers that to OKLCH spacing. The hex box takes any color format typed (`readColor`) and shows hex; Copy uses the last chosen format (`StudioColorCopyFormat`); a multi-format field was tried and the owner prefers the plain box. In float images an intensity chip sets stops past white (`IntensityChip`, `brightened`, `StudioColorIntensity`).
  - The tool chip names the selection tools by their shape alone (Rectangle, Lasso, Magic wand…): its icon says they select. The canvas ring's middle is the brush in use, ringed by the paint color (a click: all brushes), and a ring marks it among the slots. The panel strip's button for more panels is ⋯, not a "+", which sat over the layers' own "+ New layer".
  - The brush picker is a list like the brush panel's; a map of brushes by softness and texture was tried and left out (no names, places that mean little).
  - Pickers (`widgets/KisStudioPicker`: effects, brushes, blend modes, tips, patterns, panels, settings; a tile's name gets two lines of its smaller font, `tileLabelHeight`) show the user's own first, everything else below in one list; no "All N"/"Only yours" link (the owner dislikes two-click reveals). Groups work as tags, stored per picker (`Source::groupsKey`, e.g. `StudioBlendModeGroups` JSON with `…Yours` and `…Version`): Krita's categories start as groups (`initialGroups`, `initialGroupsOf`), the rest under Other; right-click toggles groups, titles rename in place and drag; empty groups go. The owner wants no ready-made groups beyond Krita's categories.
  - Decimal values show no trailing zeros (192, not 192.00), and a drag lands by size: whole from 10 up, tenths from 1, hundredths below, never coarser than a 200th of the range (`KisValueSteps`, in Krita's slider boxes and the effect panel). Shift drags finely, Ctrl in steps; typed values stay as typed.
  - One law for a value dragged with nothing drawn under the pointer (the top bar's scrubbers, drags on the image for effects, Krita's effect knobs, the hold-and-drag action, new image sizes, the time-lapse length, the color intensity chip): `KisValueSteps::draggedBy`/`turnedBy`. A size doubles every 100 px; anything else crosses its usual range in 200 px; a wheel notch is a fifth of a doubling or a twentieth of the range; Shift is a quarter as fast. These are the top bar's own gains. A drag over a drawn bar (value rows, layer rows' opacity, brush editor rows, the smoothing fill on the tool chip) moves the bar's edge with the pointer instead.
  - Value view (the zoom pill's grayscale): a press shows it at once; let go quickly it stays on, held it was a look and goes off; a click while on turns it off (`KisStudioCanvasHud::eventFilter`, the button keeping its action for the tooltip and key). Its tooltip is just Grayscale and the key: the owner found gesture lines there noise.
  - Images dropped on the canvas (`KisView::dropEvent`): in the Studio, on the image a new layer (the file itself when there is one, never Krita's paste-format question), beside it a reference image; Alt keeps Krita's menu of every way (file layer, new document).
  - Clip to layers below (`studio_clip_below`, made in `KisStudioLayout`): Krita's alpha inheritance as a switch showing the current layer's, on the top bar after alpha lock for painting tools (offered once to bars arranged before, `StudioBarClipOffered`). It clips to everything below in the group, as Krita's does.
  - Push: Krita's `KisToolTransformLiquify` action, named Push in the Studio (`tidyCommand`) with its own icon (`studio_push`, a swirl), first among the rail's tools, under its first space (offered once, `StudioRailPushOffered`). On a layer with nothing on it, Push puts the tool away, picks the topmost visible layer with paint and Pushes again (a layer picked under the transform tool restarts it in Move), or says Nothing to push.
  - Painting an effect (`KisStudioEffectBrush`, the effect panel's brush button, `paintIn`): the effect becomes a filter mask showing nowhere, picked once Krita has added it (picking it at once failed the layer panel's assert), and the brush paints it in, white (the paint color comes back after), the eraser taking it out. A bar over the canvas: Apply bakes it into the layer's pixels where painted (the filter run through the mask's selection in one undo step with the mask's removal; paint layers only), Keep leaves the mask, Drop removes it; picking another layer keeps it.
  - Colors pinned to the rail (`color:#rrggbb`): a color dropped on the rail (a swatch, the color chip) becomes a round swatch the color chip's size, never lit (a color is picked, not a place to be in); a click makes it the paint color, a right click opens its picker (`KisStudioColorField::openPicker`, the field the rail's own child so remaking the buttons leaves it) and the pin takes the color once the picker rests, dragged off it goes. A right click on the color chip opens the picker of the color under it, front or back. A pinned color dragged onto the image fills as a swatch does (the drag carries the color too and copies, so the pin stays) and has the chip's tooltip. A pinned color dropped anywhere but the image itself (where it fills, nothing over it: the canvas widget spans the window, under the panels and around the picture) or the rail goes, on a panel that takes colors too.
  - The selection tools' bar ends with Deselect and Invert, always there (enabled with a selection): shown only with one, the bar changed width and ran under the window's buttons.
  - Input tokens: a press is a drag once it moves `KisModernStyle::dragDistance(event)` (Windows' drag distance, 4 px, twice that for a pen or a finger; never `QApplication::startDragDistance()`, which Krita sets to 25 for its item views); a press on a value (a slider, a scrubber, a brush row) drags past `KisModernStyle::scrubDistance` (half that, at least 2 px) and counts from the press, so nothing is lost to the threshold; a press that must choose between two directions (a layer row: sideways opacity, up or down to move it) decides at three times that; a key, press or finger is held once down for `KisModernStyle::holdInterval()` (Windows' key repeat delay, 500 ms by default): hold-to-revert keys, the rings, touch mode's long press, the pickers' long press. Effects' drags on the image wait for the drag distance in the overlay, except a middle's; a drag counts from where it passed the distance, so a pen doesn't jump.
  - Zoom steps are even by ratio, each caught by a simple ratio (`KoZoomMode::generateStandardZoomLevels`): 100, 125, 150, 200…, 80, 67, 50… (`TestKoZoomAction` fails by design).
  - Popups opened from popups: the Studio's popups are tool windows (true popups are black on Windows), which stack by activation among the main window's; one opened while another is pressed or active is owned by it (`PopupToggle::own` in `KisPopupCloser`), which Windows keeps above, and given back to its first owner when either hides (Windows destroys what an owner owns with it).
  - Tooltips (`KisStudioTips`): the name, then each gesture as a little mouse picture and a word (`KisStudioTips::html`; written in code as "Drag: change\nRight-click: reset"), never colon-and-line text blocks. Sized to their text up to 380 px (`sized`), placed beside small controls (`placeBeside`), hidden when they only repeat the button's words, all off with `StudioTooltips`; how soon they show is `StudioTooltipDelay` (the style's wake-up delay, 350 ms by default). They keep out of the way (`TipFilter`): none for the delay after a click, stroke, wheel or key (the control just used stays quiet; another waits for the pointer to rest), the delay being the setting and nothing hidden on top (tips seen often waited 1.1 s longer, and the owner found the setting a lie), each sits beside its control or list row toward the window's middle and off the canvas around the last stroke, and fades when the pointer moves 8 px. Jargon choices carry one-line tips (`KisStudioPenPopup::inputTip`, `KisStudioBrushEditor::optionTip`). The brush tooltip is a card (`resourcewidgets/KisIconToolTip`).
  - Touch mode (`KisTouchMode`) shows what otherwise appears only on hover; a finger held still after a drag isn't a reset; menus open on the context menu event. A long press arrives as a plain right click: `longPressAt` on `qApp` tells them apart.
  - Skissa's brush set (`krita/data/skissa_brushes`, made by `packaging/skissa-brushes.py` from Krita's default bundle, Krita's MyPaint pencil and Rakurri's set): Round, Opacity, Flow, Size, Soft, Pencil, Rough, Blend, Blur, Smear, Fill, Clone, each the original under a short name (Flow with the owner's settings, after their CSP brush; Smear is Blender Basic smearing, since Krita's Blender Smear was made for the smudge engine of before 5.0 and smears nothing in ours). A one-time step (`KisSettingsTransfer::useSkissaBrushes`, `StudioBrushSet`) imports them and the bundles shipped with Skissa into the profile (Krita copies what is installed only at a profile's first start), puts every other brush away (the user's own folder kept unless named like Krita's "b)_" copies), the bundles' tags too, and sets All's order. Put away is a list in kritarc (`KisResourceOrder::putAway`, undoable), not inactive: All leaves those out while nothing is searched for (`KisTagFilterResourceProxyModel::setHidesPutAway`, set by the brush list), search and the picker's search find them, and All's "+" shows every brush, a click putting it in the set or away; while adding, the + is a ✓ that ends it, with Import… beside it (`KisTagChooserWidget::setAdding`), no row of its own. Version 5 made them inactive, which the owner disliked: version 6 makes them active again and puts them away. Rakurri's bundle (CC BY-SA, credited in NOTICE) is 177 MB: it is kept beside the build in `krita-deps/bundles` (`SKISSA_EXTRA_BUNDLES`) and installed from there. The brush picker shows the brushes' pictures as small tiles, no Recent row, in the list's order. A faint stroke (watercolors, low flow) is brightened in the list, not dropped as painting nothing. Engines that paint nothing on an empty layer get a stroke drawn in the same style instead of their old icon (`drawnStroke`): a filled lasso for the shape filler (Fill), checks along the wave for Clone, a soft stroke for a filter brush, a stroke running out for a deformer. A brush's tip is a small card: a stroke above the name, or a square picture beside it.
  - The top bar's Size and Opacity scrubbers show the brush's dab under them while they change (`KisStudioScrubber::setLook`, `DabView`): as large as the canvas shows it (scaled down past 200 px, its ring dashed), in the color and opacity, on checks when see-through, with no panel behind it, only a dark and a light ring; up to 60% of the screen's shorter side, then dashed.
  - Krita's hue, saturation and lightness blend modes in HSY, HSI, HSL and HSV are left out of the pickers, Find and the modes a scrub steps through (`KoMixing::isRetired`); the OKLCH ones do that job. Layers and files keep theirs.
  - Brushes as strokes (`widgets/KisPresetStrokeThumbnails`, `kis_preset_chooser.cpp`): each brush paints a wave, after its picture (`PresetStrokePictures`); a brush that paints nothing there (blenders) is repainted with full color rate and no smudging, fading out. With Names on strokes off (`PresetStrokeNames`) a row is its stroke alone, centered. Strokes flow into as many columns as fit comfortably (`KisPresetChooser::compactStrokeRows`, a grid set on the view itself, a little short of the whole width or Qt wraps a row that fills it exactly). The brush picker (`KisStudioBrushPicker`) follows the same setting (pictures when off); it shows the lists' own strokes (one cache, the text's ink on the panel, no backdrops); the canvas ring takes them in black for its paper (`strokePicture`). Thumbnails paint as plain paint (`KisResourcesSnapshot::setCompositeOpId(COMPOSITE_OVER)`, no alpha lock): with an erasing brush in use every stroke came out empty and was remembered as painting nothing. The picker's groups start as Krita's tags, each brush under its first in the tag chooser's order, untagged ones under Other; groups a user saved stay. Backdrops to smear were tried; the owner found them loaded and hard to read.
  - Hangs of 10 s log the main thread's stack to `krita-hang.log` in the profile folder (`krita/KisHangWatch`); a hang leaves nothing in the crash log. `KRITA_DEV_STALLS=<ms>` logs shorter stalls to `krita-stalls.log`. Stack walking: `libs/global/KisStackWalk`.
  - Autosave waits for a pause in work, at most a minute (`KisDocument`). Autosaves the user turned down ("Don't save" on close, "Open saved version" on Home, declined at start) go to the Recycle Bin, not deleted. So an autosave newer than its image is unsaved work, and opening the image opens it, marked unsaved, without asking (`KisNewerAutosave::of`, in `KisDocument::openPath`; Krita's Autosave or Saved file dialog, `KisRecoverNamedAutosaveDialog`, is left unused for merges, which the owner never liked); an own autosave older than its image goes to the Recycle Bin once the image has opened. Home shows such an image by its autosave's picture and time with the "•" of unsaved work, and its right click offers "Open saved version" while it isn't open. Programmatic undo (a dialog's Cancel taking back its layer) uses `KisSettingsUndo::DocumentOnly`, or it can take back a setting instead. Backups (`name.kra~`) go to the Temp folder by default (`backupfilelocation` 2), the owner's choice, though Windows cleans it and same-named files share a backup: the profile's own, `<Temp>/<name>/backups` (`KisProfile::tempFolder`). Per-layer stroke logs are saved in .kra (`image/KisStrokeLog`). Krita reopens the last image at start, guarded against crash loops.
  - Frame pacing for VRR screens (`KisUpdatePacer`); on ANGLE Qt composes after a flush, not a finish (`composeWithoutWaiting`).
  - Every UI change should reach the screen at its full rate (high-refresh screens included). Qt repaints every widget over a GL canvas whenever the canvas is dirty in the same frame as any other widget, though they hold none of its pixels (7–10 ms a frame with panels open): the pacer shows a changed canvas in a frame of its own after the widgets (`KisUpdatePacer::presentCanvas`), where Qt draws it alone. The pacer slows by a refresh at a time (to every fourth at most) only when half of the last sixteen frames were late. Things a drag changes at every step must repaint only what changed: chips set again unchanged aren't repainted (`Chip::pictureKey`), an effect panel's row refreshes only visibility and tiles, its overlay doesn't raise itself when on top (a raise repaints it whole), and pictures made from the layer wait for a pause (Fewer colors). A value row's bar follows the pointer, not the value's steps (`m_dragged`): stepped, it read as low fps. Measure with `KRITA_DEV_FRAMELOG` (`shown` and `canvas shown` lines, each paint with its widget and rect); dev input drags must arrive synchronously (`KritaDevMouse`), or posted messages starve Qt's own events.
  - Krita keeps a tool manager per window: reach it through `KisStudioTools` and connect each part to its own window's.
  - Keys belong to the canvas (B, E, Space, Tab are the painter's): a field done with (Enter, Esc) and a panel, flyout or inline edit hiding while it holds the focus give it back with `KisStudioTools::focusCanvas`, not to whatever Qt picks next. A slider box counts as a text input only while its text is being typed (`isTextInput` in `kis_input_manager_p.cpp`); buttons and switches in panels take no focus.
  - Overlays stay on top with `KisStudioLayout::keepAbove`; raise only what is being dragged (two overlays raising themselves swap and repaint forever).
  - Shared helpers, one copy each: sRGB and Oklab (`KisColorMath.h`), color mix (`KisModernStyle::mix`), search scores (`KisStudioPicker::matchScore`), popup shadow room (`KisStudioGlass::shadowMargin`). Look for one before writing another. Older copies remain (Oklab in the composite ops, glass, Color, Drawing and mixing; Find's and Settings' own scores): fold them in when touching that code.
- **Palette panel** (`plugins/dockers/skissa_palette`): no add button; a color dragged in from anywhere (the color chip, the recent row, another palette) goes in the empty slot under it or the dashed slot after the last swatch (tooltip "Drop a color here"); swatches drag to move, off the panel to delete. Empty places are outlined, never filled (a fill read as a pale color on glass); every swatch has a faint edge for white and pastels on light glass; the recent row is round, drops of paint as on the rail.
- **Canvas scaling, Best** (`krita/data/shaders/highq_downscale.frag`, `filterLanczos`): below 200% Krita's "High quality" is a Lanczos-3 filter (magnified, on the full-size texels; Krita had bilinear from 100%) of the finest mipmap level at most twice the screen's size, adjacent same-sign taps read as one linear fetch (about 7 × 7 reads at most). The kernel stays inside the texels each tile shares with its neighbors (`texelBorder`, 16 at full size, halving per level), so it is cut short from 25% down rather than show seams. Measured against reference downscales: Krita's old filter matched an area average and Lanczos-2 a bicubic, both equally soft; Lanczos-3 is clearly sharper at 25%, close to the old one at 33%. From 200% every mode shows plain pixels.
