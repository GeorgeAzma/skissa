# Small systems

Part of CLAUDE.md, moved out to keep every session's context small. Read it before working in this area; keep it current.

  - Colors in panels and Settings are `widgets/KisStudioColorField`: a swatch whose click opens the color panel's picker (`KisStudioColorWheel`) with the color written out (any format `KisStudioColorWheel::colorOfText` reads), the paint colors, an eyedropper and, where it may be see-through, Opacity; right-click resets. Never Krita's color dialog. The eyedropper (`KisStudioColorDropper`, a `qApp` filter) judges the canvas by the widget an event goes to, not `QApplication::widgetAt` (which asks Windows and finds another program's window whenever one is in front), reads only inside the image, and takes what the owner's sampler gives (effects: the layer as it was, not the preview).
  - Studio popups (`widgets/KisStudioPopup`): a glass panel in its own window, closed by a press elsewhere unless pinned; popups opened from it count as inside (`KisPopupCloser`). A plain `QWidget` (exactly that class, by `typeid`, without mouse tracking) counts as background that drags the window (not a scroll area's viewport: a list's first click went to dragging, and only a quick second one, a double click, picked) (`KisStudioGlass::setMovableByBackground`): give interactive widgets their own class. Checking the meta object counted classes without `Q_OBJECT` as plain, so the color picker and swatches in a color field's popup dragged the window on a click and picked only on a double click. Popups and dragged dialogs stay on screen (`DialogMover::onScreen`).
  - Toggling: a press on a popup's anchor closes it and is swallowed, so buttons toggle their popups (`KisPopupCloser::installToggle`, for every popup and menu; `popupBeside` passes the anchor). A press within 150 ms after one closed it, and Qt's double click, are swallowed too. Only closing by the anchor arms this (arming on every close swallowed fast clicks between buttons); input-less windows (a floating message) aren't popups. Buttons need no guards of their own. The owner finds a second click that reopens a popup obviously wrong.

  - Layer rows (`plugins/dockers/skissa_layers`: `LayerBox`, `NodeDelegate`): a sideways drag is the opacity (no slider); blend mode and opacity show only when unusual, on hover for the current row, always in touch mode (`updateRowShown`); double-click types, right-click makes it full (`opacityRect`). Let go at 0%, the layer is hidden instead, keeping its opacity, as one undo step. Isolate (`isolate_active_layer`, `) with several layers chosen shows those alone, with their groups and what they hold (`KisStudioSolo`, from `KisNodeManager::changeIsolationMode`): the others are only left out of the picture (`setTemporaryHiddenFromRendering`, in a stroke, then the graph refreshed), nothing in the file or the history; picking one of them keeps it, any other layer or the key again ends it, and a copy made for saving is drawn again whole (`redrawForSaving`, in `lockAndCloneImpl`). A thumbnail held still (`holdInterval`) shows its layer alone until let go, however the pointer drifts meanwhile (Krita's isolate mode, `NodeView` `soloTimer`; a move first is a drag); Ctrl+click on it selects its pixels (Krita's); a double-click on it opens the layer's properties, which open beside the pointer with nothing typed in yet, and pointing back at the canvas gives it the keys again (`CanvasSwitcher::takeKeysFromToolWindow`, for any tool window whose focus isn't typing), so Space pans while they are open. A press on the row's blend mode is passed to the row, so a sideways drag there is the opacity; only a click opens the modes. A row's switches (lock, clip, alpha…) flip when let go where pressed, so a sideways drag from one is the opacity too. In tall rows the switches and the eye sit in the row's middle and the second line ends before the switches; the mode box's margins go outside that line (its text is the line's height). A tag being renamed isn't drawn under its field (`KisTagItemDelegate::renaming`), as a layer's name isn't. A filter layer's properties open in the same Studio panel as a filter mask's (`KisStudioMaskEditor`). A layer's right click is its card (`LayerCard`, a `KisStudioPopup` beside the panel, level with the row; a right click on empty rows shows its list version: New, Paste, Find a layer, Select layers and List, the list's look), holding only what can't be done on the row by hand (the owner turned down one with the name, blend mode, opacity and switches, and words everywhere): Duplicate, Merge down, Group (Ungroup on a group), Copy, Paste and Delete as icons always in those places, named with their keys in tooltips (`runsAction`); the color labels under them; then New, Select its pixels, Style and More as rows, the menus opening beside the card, their commands run once the menu and the card are gone. In the Studio a paint, group, vector or clone layer's properties (the command, a double-click on its picture) are its card too: everything Krita's dialog held is on the row; filter, fill and file layers and masks keep their own editors.
  - Default keys (`libs/widgetutils/KisSkissaShortcuts.h`): one short list is every default; any other action starts with none (Find, its card or the Keys page give it one). - **Canvas scaling, Best** (`krita/data/shaders/highq_downscale.frag`, `filterLanczos`): below 200% Krita's "High quality" is a Lanczos-3 filter (magnified, on the full-size texels; Krita had bilinear from 100%) of the finest mipmap level at most twice the screen's size, adjacent same-sign taps read as one linear fetch (about 7 × 7 reads at most). The kernel stays inside the texels each tile shares with its neighbors (`texelBorder`, 16 at full size, halving per level), so it is cut short from 25% down rather than show seams. Measured against reference downscales: Krita's old filter matched an area average and Lanczos-2 a bicubic, both equally soft; Lanczos-3 is clearly sharper at 25%, close to the old one at 33%. From 200% every mode shows plain pixels.

Krita's Foreground to Background and Foreground to Transparent are the gradients that follow the current colors.

The painting engine (brush pyramids, textures, spray), the canvas and glass backdrops keep their own scaling.
- **Gestures** (`KisStudioGestures`, the `studio_gestures` command on the image page and in Find, which knows "how", "help", "tips"): what no button shows (drags, holds, right clicks) as little looping drawings painted in code in the theme's ink, not recordings: captures miss canvas decorations and a clip would need redoing on every look change. Each is a `Scene`, a function of the moment in a 3.2 s round; a clip draws only while shown. Add one when a gesture is added.
- **Command previews** (`KisStudioCommandPreview`, a `qApp` filter on menus): while a menu entry is lit, Crop to the selection (`resizeimagetoselection`) dims what would go and the fill-selection commands paint the selection in their color, as a canvas decoration (`studioCommandPreview`). Short entries are matched through `kisShortcutTarget`. Flatten and Merge down look the same before and after, so they show nothing.
- **Plain number boxes** (`SpinScrub` in `KisModernStyle.cpp`, attached in `polish`): every `QSpinBox`/`QDoubleSpinBox` but Krita's slider boxes drags sideways by `KisValueSteps::draggedBy` (a range from 0 over 1000 wide is a size, by ratios; others across at most 200 per 200 px; Shift finer), a right press puts back the value it had when first shown (`skissaOpenedWith`), a click without a drag types (all selected; once typing, a click places the caret). Dialogs focus their first box on opening, so focus alone doesn't mean typing (`skissaTyping`).
- **Group titles** lose a trailing "options" in English (`KisSentenceCase::groupTitle`): "Geometry options" reads Geometry, "Options" alone shows nothing.
