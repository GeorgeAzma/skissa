# Studio layout, panels and start page

Part of CLAUDE.md, moved out to keep every session's context small. Read it before working in this area; keep it current.

- **Studio layout** (`KisStudioLayout`): the whole shell. Left rail (tools, color chip), right rail (panels as flyouts that pin), top bar = the current tool's context (tool chip with the brush name, the tool's options, size/opacity, brush and blend mode), then its buttons (`KisStudioBarButtons`, `StudioBarItems`: symmetry, shapes, eraser, alpha lock, reload by default; any command can be added). Canvas-only mode brings the bars back while the pointer is at a canvas edge (`peek`; the top edge brings the menu and window buttons too, the way out of the mode; nothing comes or goes while a button or the pen is down, so a stroke can pass an edge). The classic layout is the `view_studio_layout` toggle.
  - Placement: the rail, the top bar and each panel group move by their grip (a tap tucks them away). The rail, the top bar and the zoom pill glide where they go (`KisStudioGlide`: at once while hidden, just shown, after a resize and where the pointer takes them; a catch or making way glides), as the groups do. Places are kept as shares of the room they can move in (`shareOf`, `StudioLayout*Spot` = "across,down,height", `m_refHeight`), catching on edges, corners and the middle (20 px, the middle 10; more catches too eagerly). The room is taken as if the window filled its screen (`roomGrowth`, not from the window's state, which Qt updates after the resize), so a smaller window keeps everything at its distance from the nearer edge: plain shares crept toward the edges and pushed groups into each other. A group keeps its top as it grows or folds (against the bottom, its bottom). A group against its edge is docked (the canvas makes room), elsewhere it floats. Groups keep off the rail (`railRectProperty`), the top bar and window buttons (`barRects`) and each other: an overlapping group moves to the nearest place clear of all of them at once (`clearOfOthers`, `settledCount`; the one moved last makes way, even while dragged) and glides there (`glideTo`); the owner wants to see panels move, not jump. The top bar at the top or bottom pushes the view (`studioCanvasBottomInset`); the S window handle stays top left and the rail keeps its gap under it (`handleBottomProperty`).
  - Lanes: a group is one glass pill of lanes side by side, each a stack of panels (`StudioLayoutPanelLanes`, widths in `<width key>Lanes`). A group dropped by another's side joins it as lanes; a single lane dropped at a lane's top or bottom joins that stack (`joinAt`, `joinGroups`). The line between lanes resizes on drag and splits on click (`LaneDivider`, `separateLanes`); a tap between stacked panels splits off those below (`separateBelow`). Joined groups as separate pills kept in step were tried and broke tucking and moving panels: one widget can't come apart. A grip tap folds the group's panels (`foldGroup`); a group's only panel has no title bar unless folded.
- **Panel columns** (`KisStudioPanelColumn`, `PanelStack` in `KisStudioLayout.cpp`): a panel opened from its button and not pinned is a flyout, closed by a press elsewhere in the interface, its button or Escape, but not by painting (`isCanvas`: the owner paints with panels open). First start pins Color, Brushes and Layers: Color and Brushes as a group at the bottom left beside the rail, Layers with the panel buttons (the owner's own layout, mostly). Group 0 is the column with the panel buttons, 1 the one across (`StudioLayoutFarPanels`), 2+ floating groups made by dropping a panel's grip on the canvas (`newPanelGroup`, `StudioLayoutPanelGroups`, `StudioLayoutPanelGroup<n>Spot/Width/Side`); an empty group goes. A scrolling panel is fitted to its contents once (160–360 px, `settled`) and keeps that height, so a growing list scrolls; the line under each panel resizes it (`StudioLayoutPanelSizes`; right-click fits it again). Resource lists stay lists (`keepUpright`). A resource's right click toggles tags in place and adds new ones (`resourcewidgets/KisResourceTagMenu`); over a list, the tag chooser (`KisTagChooserWidget`) and a magnifier that opens into search (`KisTagFilterWidget`). Profiles start with no tags: the shipped ones are made inactive once (`KisSettingsTransfer::startWithoutTags`, `StudioTagsCleared`). The brush list hides All and All untagged while there are tags and All untagged always (`setHidesAllRows`, counting the tags listed, not the database's); tags drag up and down their list, brushes drag within their tag or All (the list's own drag, pictures or strokes; out of the list it becomes the drag that pins to the tools), both orders kept in kritarc, not the database (`KisResourceOrder`, settings `StudioTagOrder`, `StudioTagResourceOrder`, undoable), so the database stays upstream's. The + beside the tag lists every brush, the tag's checked and the rest faded, a click putting one in or taking it out, with Import… (kpp into the tag, bundles and abr as storages) and Done; brush files dropped on the list import too (`widgets/KisBrushListArranger`).
- **Zoom pill** (`KisStudioCanvasHud`): zoom (click: 100%, from 100% to fit), value view, mirror, rotation and the modes that are on. It goes anywhere free on the canvas by a drag (`StudioLayoutViewPillSpot`, `KisStudioLayout::spotShare`), keeping off bars and groups (`studioTarget`). It holds its own `KisStudioBarButtons` (`StudioViewPillItems`, none by default; buttons drag between it and the top bar); its right click adds buttons or hides it (`StudioViewPill`, in Settings' Zoom card).
- **Start page** (`KisStudioStartPage`): with no image open the Studio shows its own page in place of Krita's welcome page. Over the last image, blurred: new-image tiles drawn to one scale (last size, screen, square, A4, 4K, portrait, clipboard), Custom, Open, then the last image large (Continue) and the others under Today, This week and Earlier (right-click: Forget), plus the images open now (unsaved too). Typing filters, arrows and Enter pick, dropped files open. Pictures are read on two threads from the files (`Pictures`; a .kra's merged image) and kept scaled on disk (`start-pictures` in the config folder, the 40 used last, JPEG when opaque), so a large .kra isn't decoded at every start. A picture dropped without a file (from a browser) opens as a new untitled image; files open as themselves. Footer: three quiet links (`FootLink`: Support Krita, Skissa, Report a problem), never a banner.
  - Custom (`CustomImage`): the sheet pulled by its corner among other sizes' outlines (`SizeStage`, catching lightly: 6 px on sizes, 4 px on ratios; Shift free; catching from far felt clumsy, none made aiming hard), numbers in px/cm/mm/in (`SizeNumbers`), shape chips that hold and size chips by the long side (`NewImage::steps`), resolution (`roundDpi`), color rows, paper colors, Make. Every new image has one layer; the owner wants no controls past those and no Krita dialog glued on.
  - Home: a click on the S handle (`WindowHandle`: drag moves the window; no double click, since its first click already goes Home) or Find's Home shows it over open images (`KisStudioLayout::showHome`); Esc or the S again goes back.
