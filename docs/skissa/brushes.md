# Brushes

Part of CLAUDE.md, moved out to keep every session's context small. Read it before working in this area; keep it current.

- **Brush editor rows** (`kis_paintop_option`, `kis_paintop_settings_widget`, `kis_categorized_item_delegate`, `plugins/paintops/libpaintop`): each option's row shows its strength (`KisPaintOpOption::RowValue`): drag, double-click to type, right-click for the value from when the brush was picked (`rememberRowValues`); 100% is hidden until hover; the lock is an icon. The Size row is the diameter. "The pen is on" means a sensor is ticked; there is no Enable Pen Settings checkbox (`rowCheckedLens`, `sensorsLens`). Opacity and Flow get a row checkbox for the pen; MyPaint rows don't (MyPaint drops input curves saved with the pen off). Saved curves (`KisCurvePresetStrip`) are global in `[SavedCurves]`.
- **Quick brush editor** (`KisStudioBrushEditor`, `KisStudioPenPopup`, `plugins/paintops/libpaintop/KisBrushTipEditor`):
  - Opens from the brush chip on the top bar, its action, or a second click on the active Paint/Blend/Erase slot. A test stroke, the brush's parts as tabs (Tip, Mask, Texture, Color; then Stroke), each with pictures and its settings as rows; "+ Add" shows the rest as tiles of dabs (`widgets/KisStudioDabPictures`). Choices are pictures (`KisStudioChips::setTileColumns`).
  - It is the only brush editor (a wide sheet of every part side by side wasted room and repeated it). Color starts with the blend mode (`blendRow`, but not for MyPaint, whose blend mode the box turns off, `blends`) and the mixing; Stroke is the last part, and smoothing changed there repaints the last stroke.
  - The header's ⋯: Save, Save as new, New brush (an engine), Copy as picture (the editor without its buttons), Classic editor (Krita's).
  - A part's options without a row show after its rows as "Name ›" chips opening Krita's own page beside the editor (`LentPage`, `lendPage`/`returnPage`; not those whose quick page is whole, `quickPageIsWhole`, nor the blend mode), not while the part is off.
  - The Pen tab (`penPart`, before Stroke): a grid of settings × pen inputs (`PenGrid`, extra inputs kept in `[StudioBrushSheet] PenColumns`): click turns an input on, click again opens its curve beside the editor, right click turns it off; "+" adds inputs and settings.
  - Settings at their engine's own default (`RowValue::neutral`: MyPaint's, from libmypaint) and not driven by the pen are offered under Add, not as rows (a MyPaint brush showed all ~30 of its settings); size, hardness and opacity always show.
  - It edits the paintop box's live option widgets (`KisPaintopBox::currentOptionWidget()`), taking a use of them (`KisPaintopBox::useOptionWidget`); each user takes and gives back its own use.
  - An option shows itself through `KisPaintOpOption`: `rowValue()`, `penInputs()`/`setPenInputActive`/`setPenCurve`/`penMode`, `createPenInputPage`, `hasQuickPage`/`createQuickPage(category)`.
  - The tip editor draws the real dab (`KisAutoBrushFactory`) with handles for size, softness (`fadeSpot`) and angle; under it the edge (`EdgeCanvas`): softness as one measure for every shape (`hardnessOf`/`setHardness`) and three shapes, Round, Smooth and Curve (`heldAt`); pressing the line makes it a curve. Handles are white with a dark ring and lines dark under light, so nothing disappears on any dab. Krita stores a taller tip turned a quarter. Angle selectors have no flip buttons.
  - Size and Spacing rows are the tip's (`KisBrushBasedPaintopOptionWidget::paintOpOptionAdded`); Spacing has its Auto on the row (`setAutomatic`, `RowValue::automatic`). A switch belongs on the row of the value it frees.
  - Re-stroke (`KisLastStrokeReplay`): while an editor is open, a brush change repaints the last stroke of that brush (recorded by the freehand helper; its step replaced with `setIndex`, not `undo()`, which takes settings back first), only while that step is newest. The stroke's step is the next one pushed after it with the stroke's name (`stepText`), never just whatever lands at the next index: a stroke taken back before its step arrived made the next action "the stroke", and a replay then deleted it. A repaint is never cancelled for a newer look; the next waits for its step: once a repaint starts, the step it replaces is gone, so stopped halfway the stroke was in no look at all. Smoothing changes replay from the pen's own path. The stroke's brush is written out only once an editor watches (`watch`), not at every stroke end. When testing settings by painting, paint a throwaway dot before each switch.
  - A row that is an optional effect (Rotation, Scatter) comes off by its cross or pulled up or down off the editor; pulled onto the top bar it is pinned there. Sideways is always the value.
- **Smoothing** (`KisStudioSmoothing`): the brush tool's smoothing as one amount, shown as a fill behind the brush name on the tool chip: drag changes it, right click resets, click opens the editor. Per brush in `[StudioBrushSmoothing]` by file, only for brushes whose smoothing was changed; the rest share one value (`<shared>`): picking or leaving a brush never gives it one. The stabilizer evens pressure and tilt over ~25 ms only (`stabilizedSensorsReach`; Krita's whole window flattened quick strokes). Held W and a sideways drag change it too (`KisDragValueAction`).
- **Stroke tab** (`KisStudioStrokePage`): the editor's last tab for freehand tools: a wobbly line run through Krita's weighted and stabilizer math, kinds as pills (Off is Krita's Basic), Strength (one value: slow strokes keep the proportion to fast ones Krita's two distances had; always measured on screen), Pixel perfect for pixel brushes, Shapes, snapping to guides. Dyna and Multibrush get Tool options for their own. Smooth pressure is a strength (0–100 %, with any smoothing or none, `KisSmoothingOptions::pressureStrength`, key `LineSmoothingPressureStrength`): the pen's pressure evened over time by a one-pole filter reaching up to 100 ms (`pressureFollow`, in `KisToolFreehandHelper::paint` before any line smoothing; the weighted kind no longer averages pressure itself). Krita's switch reads as 50 % and is written as on whenever it is above 0; a brush remembers it as `s<strength>`.
- **Brush tweaks across a restart** (`KisStudioBrushTweaks`): with Keep brush tweaks on, dirty brushes' settings go to `brush-tweaks.json` in the profile folder (keyed by file and md5; only brushes changed since are serialized, the file written on a thread of its own) and come back at the first window unless the file changed. Brush files are never written. Pick a brush through `KisPaintopBox::resourceSelected`, as Krita's panel does: `setPaintOpPreset` alone leaves the settings widget on the old engine.
- **Pinned controls** (`KisStudioControls`): a brush setting (`brushsetting:<option id>`) or a Krita setting (`setting:<registry id>`) as a row on the top bar; pins show on hover, rows are pulled to move or remove them. Fractions show as percent.
