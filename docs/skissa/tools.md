# Tools

Part of CLAUDE.md, moved out to keep every session's context small. Read it before working in this area; keep it current.

- **Canvas size** (`KisStudioCanvasSize`, `KisCanvasSizeCatch`, `KisToolCrop::beginCanvasResize`): in the Studio, Canvas size is the Crop tool's Canvas kind on the image: fitted with room around, handles on the canvas's edges at once, presses off them ignored, the gained room shown as checkers, edges catching on the size it had and usual sizes (6 screen px) and ratios (4), Shift keeps its shape, Alt both sides, Enter or Esc back to the tool before.
- **Line tool** (`plugins/tools/basictools/KisToolCurveStroke`, named Line, in the Shape slot after Shape; Krita's Line, Polygon, Polyline, Freehand path and Calligraphy are out of the slot and Find, Polygon being Shape's Sides, the Shape slot keeping Shape, Line, Bezier, Edit shapes and Comic panels): a drag draws a line tapered at both ends (a third of it each) and the stroke stays open: a press on any point of it bends it there (a quadratic through the point held, no handles), near its ends moves an end; a press away from it keeps it and starts the next, Enter or another tool keeps it, Escape or undo drops it. While dragged the brush paints it off the image, into a device of its own once per turn of the event loop (`paintPreview`, a `KisPainter` with the preset), drawn as a tool decoration (its shape in the paint color for a brush that paints nothing alone, smudging); the brush paints it on the image once let go, the preview staying until the image has no updates left: repainting the image stroke at every move cancelled and redrew it and flashed. Captures don't show the preview (a QPainter decoration). Starting a stroke asks the others to end (`requestsOtherStrokesToEnd`): the tool ignores that request while its own repaint starts one.
- **Shape tool** (`plugins/tools/basictools/KisToolSuperellipse`, `KisSuperellipse`): the Shape slot's first tool, in place of Krita's Rectangle and Ellipse (still built for the classic layout, left out of the slot and Find). A dragged box (Krita's rectangle base: Shift square, Ctrl from the middle, Alt moves it, Ctrl+Alt turns it) becomes one shape: a regular polygon of Sides (3 to 12, a side flat at the bottom), turned (presets only: the five-point star points down so its points are up; an Angle row was left out, since Ctrl+Alt turns the box on the canvas), stretched to the box, its corners Lamé curves in the parallelogram of the two sides meeting there, each kept inside its own wedge from the middle so corners never cross (`roundedIn`), the polygon widened until the rounded path fills the box, so corners stay round on a long box. Four sides at no turn is the box, and draws as before. Roundness is the corner reach as a share of the shortest half side, so a long box's corners are as round as a square's (all of it a pill); Stretch (a chip beside Round corners, `stretched`) makes the shape in a square and stretches it to the box, corners too, Roundness then of the longest half side: Ellipse, Diamond and the stars are stretched presets. Stretching a rounded rectangle's corners with its box read as wrong to the owner; Curve is the corners' exponent, dragged by ratios and caught only where shapes look different (tenths under 1, halves to 6, then whole: `KisStudioValueRow::Range::snapped`; hundredths read as noise to the owner) (2 round, higher squarer, 1 a cut, under 1 pinched in, a star pointing at the sides' middles, so the five-point star is a pentagon turned 36°). Presets, two rows of six (box shapes, then polygons), and Sides, Roundness and Curve beside a preview of the shape as set are in its panel; Krita's fill and outline form is kept out of sight in the Studio for the bar to mirror. Changing the look right after drawing reshapes what was drawn (`KisRedoLast`), as one step. The look is registry settings (`[KritaShape/KisToolSuperellipse] skissa*`, `KisSuperellipse::settingIds`), so Ctrl+Z takes a change back; a part dragged alone is one step, a preset's several parts one step (`setValues`), and their writes reach the tool and the rail once (`KisSuperellipse::written`). Shapes are Find items (`shape:<preset id>`, or `shape:sides,roundness,curve,angle` for one's own, resolved by `KisSuperellipse::lookOf` without an index entry), so they pin to the rail or the top bar with their settings: a preset or the preview dragged onto the rail or the bar stays there (the owner preferred that to a Pin button), and a right click opens the item card with "Pin to tools" (`KisStudioFind::showCard(key, anchor)`). A pinned shape picks the tool with its look (`KisSuperellipse::draw`) and is lit on the rail while the tool draws it (`Watch::changed`). On a vector layer it adds a path shape.
- **Shapes** (`tool/KisStrokeShape`, `KisToolFreehand`): with Shapes on, a stroke held still half a second at its end becomes a line, arc, ellipse/circle, polygon/rectangle or polyline, as one undo step (`repaintAs`, keeping pressure). Thresholds are in screen pixels, tuned so scribbles, waves and spirals never snap. The colorize mask brush opts out (`m_snapsToShapes`).
- **Time-lapse** (`plugins/dockers/skissa_recorder`): in the Studio it lives under the overview's picture (`withTimelapse` in `KisStudioPanelPages`, the panel retired): a record dot, a line dragged to show earlier frames over the picture (the preview as an overlay, `studioOverlay`), "N strokes → 30 s" with the length dragged, Save in the chosen format; ⋯ holds Record every image, the formats, Recordings…, Keep recordings in…, Video options…. A frame after each finished step (only while Skissa is the active window, so scripted tests record nothing). The frames go in at exactly frames ÷ seconds (`$IN_FPS` as a fraction), so the video is the length chosen however short or long the session. The frames folder (thousands after a long session) is listed off the window's thread. Krita's recorder and ffmpeg underneath.
  - A frame for each finished step of the history (`stepDone` in `recorder_writer.cpp`, checked every 100 ms once the image is idle), not one every few seconds: long pauses show nothing and a quick run of strokes shows each one. The capture interval menu is gone.
- **Liquify and warp** (`libs/image/KisGridRenderer`, `strokes/KisLiquifyInPlace` in the transform tool): Krita's grids drawn pixel for pixel the same, on all threads, in tiles of 128 px that each draw the cells reaching them in grid order (so folds stack as Krita's do); liquify's cells are nudged (`iterateThroughGrid`), warp's exact (`processGrid`). Warp's grid points, each a sum over every handle, are found on all threads too (7–8× faster in all). `KisGridRendererTest` compares bytes with Krita's own drawing. A move redraws only the cells whose corners moved (`KisLiquifyTransformWorker::runChanges`) and writes only that area into the layer, without undoing the last update (`KisLiquifyInPlace`; after writing twice the layer's area it writes whole again, since each write keeps its tiles for undo). Copies of a worker share its spatial containers until one changes them, and a preview level's copy scales them only if something asks (drawing never does; `KisSpatialContainer::scale` moves a tree whole): copying and rescaling the grid took 100–200 ms a move, against about 4 ms for a whole update now.
- **Perspective in Move** (`tool_transform2/KisTransformPerspective`, called from `kis_free_transform_strategy.cpp`): Ctrl-drag a corner bends the box into perspective, a middle handle moves its edge, a vanishing point (shown while Ctrl is held) moves as in Krita's perspective mode; Ctrl elsewhere is still Krita's 3D tilt. The quad is turned back into free transform values (`fit`: a turn of the plane about an in-plane axis for the projective part, the rest as scale, shear and z rotation), so it stays a FREE_TRANSFORM that upstream reads and transform masks animate. While bending, the edges are drawn faintly out to where they meet. Corners catch (`Catcher`) on the canvas corners and edges, other layers' corners, Grid guides' corners and edges, line guides, and the line from the corner's fixed neighbour to any perspective guide's vanishing point, where two cross first. Ctrl+Alt on a corner starts Flatten: the image stays as it was while the four corners are placed (then without keys), and when the last lands it is shown straight on, with the proportions the thing has in the world (`flattening`, Zhang and He's method; one vanishing point leaves the lens a guess). Flattening a part of a photo works on a selection: the whole layer often reaches past the horizon. `KisTransformPerspectiveTest` checks the fit, the proportions and the lines.
- **Bend** (`libs/image/KisPuppet`, `tool_transform2/KisBendTransformStrategy`): the transform tool's puppet warp. The mode bar shows Move, Bend and Push (liquify); perspective, warp, cage and mesh are behind ⋯, which shows the one in use (`KisStudioToolStrip::addTransformModes`). The picture is a grid of cells, stiff where there is any paint and nearly free air between, posed as rigidly as possible (Igarashi 2005: two closed-form solves, factored once per set of pins), so the same pins always give the same picture. Each separate shape moves by its own pins: without any it stays exactly (its cells set back after the solve, so it is neither resampled nor redrawn), with one it moves without turning, with more it bends; the air holds lightly to its nearest shape's movement (an air anchored to where it was dragged and turned pinned shapes). A press adds a pin and drags it, right-click removes one. The pose is written into the arguments' liquify grid (`KisLiquifyTransformWorker::setTransformedPoints`), so stroke, undo, in-place updates and files are liquify's and Krita opens the result as a liquify transform; the pins travel in the worker (`setPins`, saved as `skissa_pins` beside Krita's element). The in-place stroke hands over the original pixels as it starts (`sigSourceReady`). A strategy's `externalConfigChanged` must ask for the image to be recomputed, or undo inside the tool shows nothing.
- **Resampling** (`libs/image/KisResampling`, filters in Krita's `KisFilterStrategyRegistry`): Smooth is Lanczos3 with each new pixel kept within the range of the source pixels it covers, and at least the two nearest it (no halos at edges; kept to the two nearest alone, a thin line between them vanished when shrinking below a half), shrinking in linear light (a float copy, written back; thin bright lines keep their brightness). Pixel weighs the source pixels a new one covers (`knowsEnlargement` passes the scale to its curve, with 1/256 slack for thirds): whole-number scales copy pixels exactly, other scales keep pixels even and blend only the seams. Auto picks Pixel for pixel art (in five 256 px windows, 64 colours at most and features a pixel or a few wide: short runs of one colour between others, `looksLikePixelArt`; a flat shape or a flats layer isn't pixel art), Smooth otherwise (`autoFilterStrategy(content, rect)`). The transform tool defaults to Smooth (Bicubic migrated) and uses Pixel for a pixel-art layer for that transform only (`useFilterForNow`). Transform masks save Krita's nearest filter, Skissa's beside it (`skissaFilterId`). Scale image shows the picture's busiest part scaled each way as tiles to pick (`widgets/KisScaleFilterPreview`). No AI upscaler: the owner doesn't want one.
- **Transform and effect previews:** full resolution while dragging. Krita's low-resolution preview (`forceLodMode`, Settings' "Instant preview") is off by default for Transform and effects; Transform still takes it when what it moves is over 12 MP (`slowTransformPixels`, the moved layers' or the selection's bounds), where the full one can't follow the pointer. Effects show a coarse pass first only when the effect is slow (see effects.md, Preview passes).
- **Symmetry** (`KisStudioSymmetry`, `plugins/tools/basictools/KisStudioMultiBrush` in place of `kis_tool_multihand`): Mirror/Radial/Tile; Radial and Tile use Multibrush and return to the previous tool. Handles via `paintToolOutline`.
- **Text tool** (`plugins/tools/svgtexttool`): besides Krita's own (typing on the canvas, its border moves it), the corners of a selected text's box scale it about the opposite corner and a drag just outside one turns it about its middle, Shift by 15° (`SvgScaleRotateTextStrategy`, a `KoShapeTransformCommand`), without leaving the tool.
- **Comic panels** (Krita's Knife, `plugins/tools/tool_knife`): on a layer that isn't a vector layer the first press makes the page (`KisToolKnife::makePage`: a vector layer "Panels" with one panel 4% inside the image's edge), and drags then cut it; Alt-drag across a gutter joins the panels, whatever the mode (Krita showed a message and did nothing).
- **Freehand selection gestures** (`KisSelectionGestures`, hooked in `KisToolOutlineBase` for selections): a line held still 350 ms at its end that roughly drew a box or an ellipse snaps to the clean one (shown while held; nearly round becomes round; only a loop). Follow edges (a bar switch, `StudioSelectFollowEdges`) moves each new point across the line to the strongest lightness step within 12 screen px of it (see-through as light, so drawings on clear layers have edges; a nearer edge wins over a slightly stronger far one); it replaces the magnetic tool, gone from the Select slot (Find still has it). A click starts corners; while placing them, a corner pressed on moves and a press on a line between two adds a corner there and moves it (`m_movedCorner`), so Krita's curve selection left the slot too.
- **One lasso** (`KisToolOutlineBase::m_clickMode`): a drag draws freehand, a click starts corners (Procreate's freehand selection): each click a corner, a drag between them a freehand part, closed by a click on the first corner, a double click or Enter, Escape drops it. Polygon left the Select slot. A click no longer deselects with the lasso (Deselect is on the bar).
- **Peeks** (`KisStudioPeek`): checkable switches whose key held goes back when let go (value view, mirror M, squint S); a tap toggles. Squint is a widget over the canvas with the visible part of the projection shrunk to a sixth of the view and box blurred, refreshed while the view or image changes. Hold the pen still (450 ms, not with an airbrush) to sample a color instead of painting (`KisToolFreehand::sampleOnHold`, Krita's sampler ring). Canvas rotation by drag sticks to straight within 4°. Alt held alone shows every button's key (`KisStudioKeyHints`, from the tooltips' `shortcutOf`). A stroke started outside the selection lights its edge (`KisSelectionDecoration::flash`, even while hidden). Start page tiles drag out to other apps as files (a PNG for .kra and unsaved open images, in the temp folder's `dragged`).
