# Skissa: how we build it

This is the working guide for this fork. Read it before changing anything; it explains what we value, how the main systems fit together and how to work with the owner. **It is a living document: when you learn something that a future agent needs (a principle the owner states, a mistake worth not repeating, a system you added), add it here in the right section, briefly, and remove what has become false.**

## What this is

Skissa (Swedish: "to sketch") is a personal fork of [Krita](https://invent.kde.org/graphics/krita) (upstream: KDE). Branch `main`, pushed to the `skissa` remote's `main`. The owner is a digital artist who paints in Krita daily and wants it to feel as obvious and visual as the tablet apps (Procreate-like) without losing Krita's power. The fork rebuilds the interface ("Studio"); the painting engine, file formats and resources stay Krita's.

The owner works fast and reviews results afterwards. They mostly talk about how things look and feel, not about code.

## How to work with the owner

- **Automate; don't ask what you can decide.** Build, install, relaunch, test and commit without checking in. Ask only for decisions that are really theirs (design direction they reserved, outward-facing actions like pushing or publishing).
- **"Give me suggestions" means text only.** Don't implement suggestions until they say "implement", "do it", or pick numbers. When they pick, do exactly those.
- **Finish every task in a message**, including ones sent mid-turn. Keep a list.
- **Verify visually.** Launch the dev build and capture the window (see Dev workflow) before saying a UI change works. Say plainly what you could not verify.
- **Report like a person:** what changed, what they'll notice, what's left. Short.
- When the owner says something feels wrong, believe it and find the cause; don't argue from the code.
- Before changing a look the owner has opinions on (glass, glow), get a reference image of what they want; don't iterate on numbers blind.

## Design principles

These come from the owner's feedback over many sessions. They decide most questions.

1. **The canvas is the product.** Chrome is a cost. Everything else appears where and when it's needed and gets out of the way.
2. **Needing explanation is a design problem.** Before adding a hint or tooltip, make the control self-evident: a picture, a live preview, direct manipulation, a better default, or removing the option.
3. **No grey helper text. Ever.** No info icons, no descriptions under settings, no "click to…" labels. A short tooltip is the fallback. Grey (secondary ink) is for quiet *values or state*, never for instructions.
4. **Sentence case.** "Open recent", not "Open Recent". Krita's Title Case text is shown in sentence case in English (`KisSentenceCase`; names and acronyms keep their capitals; translations untouched): menu commands (`KisStudioLayout::tidyCommand`, which also keeps classic-only items hidden), form captions without their colon (`KisModernStyle::polish`), option names (`KisPaintOpOption::label`), widget tooltips (`KisStudioTips`). Items the user named (files, layers, brushes) never go through it.
5. **Shortest clear text.** "Layer 5", not "Paint Layer 5". Drop type names, restated context and "Click to". Most controls need no tooltip: if the name says it, leave it out. When one is needed, it's one line an artist would say, telling what the name can't ("Huge brushes paint slowly", "Nearest is best for pixel art"): no restating the name, no "X, but Y" or "…, so …", no "e.g.". A short name before ": " shows in bold above the rest (`leadOf`). A button whose name says what it does gets just the name.
6. **Direct manipulation over forms.** Drag a value on its own label or row. Handles on the canvas instead of number fields. Typing is a double-click away, in place, no box (`widgets/KisStudioInlineEdit`). **Right-click resets a value to its default.** Shift drags finer.
7. **Show, don't name.** Live previews on the real image (effects on canvas, blend modes as thumbnails of the actual picture, brush tooltips with strokes), curves as little drawings, themes as swatches.
8. **Fewer options, better defaults.** Remove settings nobody should need. Merge duplicates. Put an option where its subject is (the eraser's own size on the Size page) and also in Settings so it can be found.
9. **Organized by intent, found by search.** Group by what the artist wants ("Blur", "Darken"), not by implementation. Everything is reachable with Find (Ctrl+K), including words from other apps.
10. **Perceptual controls.** Brush size moves by ratios (log); opacity stays linear.
11. **Stable places, personal content.** Primary controls don't move. Customizing uses the same gestures as using (drag, right-click) and always has a reset.
12. **One undo.** Ctrl+Z also undoes settings changes (and rail and bar changes), interleaved with document steps by time.
13. **Stay Krita underneath.** Files and presets the fork writes must open in upstream Krita and the other way round. Never change a preset just by loading it. Keep upstream code paths intact so merges stay easy.

## Look and feel

All tokens live in `libs/widgetutils/KisModernStyle.h`. Use them; don't invent numbers.

- **Spacing:** `hairline` 2, `space1` 4, `space2` 8, `space3` 12, `space4` 16. Nothing else.
- **Corners:** squircles everywhere (`KisModernStyle::squircle`). Radii: `cornerRadius` 5 (controls), `tileRadius` 8, `cardRadius` 10, `panelRadius` 14. Checkboxes, swatches, switches and segmented controls are squircles (`fullSquircleRadius` for checkboxes). Radio buttons stay circles.
- **Motion:** one curve, an exponential ease-out (`motionCurve()`), and three durations: `enterDuration` 180 ms, `exitDuration` 120 ms, `changeDuration` 160 ms. Things that move glide, they don't jump.
- **Ink:** text in the palette's text color; quiet values in `secondaryInk()`; fills and tracks as text color at low alpha (works on any theme and on glass). The accent is optional (themes can have none).
- **Selected rows:** use `KisModernStyle::selectedRow()` / `selectedRowInk()`, never a mix toward `QPalette::Highlight`: a theme without an accent has a gray Highlight made for checks and slider fills (dark on light themes), which made selected rows a heavy mid-gray with white text.
- **Glass:** panels, popups and dialogs are frosted glass over the canvas. See Glass below before touching any panel painting.
- **Icons:** `krita/pics/svg/{dark,light}_studio_*.svg`, generated by `krita/pics/svg/make_studio_icons.py` (24 px grid, round strokes; a switch that is on is drawn solid). The app icon is a bare pencil S (white with a soft shadow on dark, ink on light; Krita names icons by their own color, so `dark_krita-branding` is the ink one). The window icon follows Windows' light/dark setting live (`taskbarIcon()` in `KisApplication`); the .exe gets the white S with a stronger shadow at small sizes. Every icon file (file icons, macOS, Android, Windows Store tiles) comes from `krita/pics/branding/make_icon.py`; the splash wordmark is `krita/data/splash/banner.svg`.

## Main systems

A map of where things are. Files are in `libs/ui/` unless noted.

- **Studio layout** (`KisStudioLayout`): the whole shell. Left rail (tools, color chip), right rail (panels as flyouts that pin), top bar = the current tool's context (tool chip with the brush name, the tool's options, size/opacity, brush and blend mode), then its buttons (`KisStudioBarButtons`, `StudioBarItems`: symmetry, shapes, eraser, alpha lock, reload by default; any command can be added). Canvas-only mode brings the bars back while the pointer is at a canvas edge (`peek`). The classic layout is the `view_studio_layout` toggle.
  - Placement: the rail, the top bar and each panel group move by their grip (a tap tucks them away). Places are kept as shares of the room they can move in (`shareOf`, `StudioLayout*Spot` = "across,down,height", `m_refHeight`), catching on edges, corners and the middle (20 px, the middle 10; more catches too eagerly). A group keeps its top as it grows or folds (against the bottom, its bottom). A group against its edge is docked (the canvas makes room), elsewhere it floats. Groups keep off the rail (`railRectProperty`), the top bar and window buttons (`barRects`) and each other: an overlapping group moves to the nearest place clear of all of them at once (`clearOfOthers`, `settledCount`; the one moved last makes way, even while dragged) and glides there (`glideTo`); the owner wants to see panels move, not jump. The top bar at the top or bottom pushes the view (`studioCanvasBottomInset`); the S window handle stays top left and the rail keeps its gap under it (`handleBottomProperty`).
  - Lanes: a group is one glass pill of lanes side by side, each a stack of panels (`StudioLayoutPanelLanes`, widths in `<width key>Lanes`). A group dropped by another's side joins it as lanes; a single lane dropped at a lane's top or bottom joins that stack (`joinAt`, `joinGroups`). The line between lanes resizes on drag and splits on click (`LaneDivider`, `separateLanes`); a tap between stacked panels splits off those below (`separateBelow`). Joined groups as separate pills kept in step were tried and broke tucking and moving panels: one widget can't come apart. A grip tap folds the group's panels (`foldGroup`); a group's only panel has no title bar unless folded.
- **Panel columns** (`KisStudioPanelColumn`, `PanelStack` in `KisStudioLayout.cpp`): a panel opened from its button and not pinned is a flyout, closed by a press elsewhere in the interface, its button or Escape, but not by painting (`isCanvas`: the owner paints with panels open). First start pins Color, Brushes and Layers. Group 0 is the column with the panel buttons, 1 the one across (`StudioLayoutFarPanels`), 2+ floating groups made by dropping a panel's grip on the canvas (`newPanelGroup`, `StudioLayoutPanelGroups`, `StudioLayoutPanelGroup<n>Spot/Width/Side`); an empty group goes. A scrolling panel is fitted to its contents once (160–360 px, `settled`) and keeps that height, so a growing list scrolls; the line under each panel resizes it (`StudioLayoutPanelSizes`; right-click fits it again). Resource lists stay lists (`keepUpright`). A resource's right click toggles tags in place and adds new ones (`resourcewidgets/KisResourceTagMenu`); over a list, the tag chooser (`KisTagChooserWidget`) and a magnifier that opens into search (`KisTagFilterWidget`).
- **Zoom pill** (`KisStudioCanvasHud`): zoom (click: 100%, from 100% to fit), value view, mirror, rotation and the modes that are on. It goes anywhere free on the canvas by a drag (`StudioLayoutViewPillSpot`, `KisStudioLayout::spotShare`), keeping off bars and groups (`studioTarget`). It holds its own `KisStudioBarButtons` (`StudioViewPillItems`, none by default; buttons drag between it and the top bar); its right click adds buttons or hides it (`StudioViewPill`, in Settings' Zoom card).
- **Start page** (`KisStudioStartPage`): with no image open the Studio shows its own page in place of Krita's welcome page. Over the last image, blurred: new-image tiles drawn to one scale (last size, screen, square, A4, 4K, portrait, clipboard), Custom, Open, then the last image large (Continue) and the others under Today, This week and Earlier (right-click: Forget), plus the images open now (unsaved too). Typing filters, arrows and Enter pick, dropped files open. Pictures are read on two threads from the files (`Pictures`; a .kra's merged image) and kept scaled on disk (`start-pictures` in the config folder, the 40 used last, JPEG when opaque), so a large .kra isn't decoded at every start. Footer: three quiet links (`FootLink`: Support Krita, Skissa, Report a problem), never a banner.
  - Custom (`CustomImage`): the sheet pulled by its corner among other sizes' outlines (`SizeStage`, catching lightly: 6 px on sizes, 4 px on ratios; Shift free; catching from far felt clumsy, none made aiming hard), numbers in px/cm/mm/in (`SizeNumbers`), shape chips that hold and size chips by the long side (`NewImage::steps`), resolution (`roundDpi`), color rows, paper colors, Make. Every new image has one layer; the owner wants no controls past those and no Krita dialog glued on.
  - Home: a click on the S handle (`WindowHandle`: drag moves the window, double click maximizes) or Find's Home shows it over open images (`KisStudioLayout::showHome`); Esc or the S again goes back.
- **Tools** (`KisStudioToolRail`, `KisStudioToolStrip`): 34 tools in 9 slots (Paint, Blend, Erase, Select, Transform, Fill, Shape, Text, Guides; Blend and Erase are the brush tool too, `toolSlots()`). Slots remember their variant; the brush in use decides whether Paint or Blend is lit. The rail's items (`StudioRailItems`: slots, single tools, spaces, Effects, and brushes pinned by dragging them from a brush list, which paint with their own size unless in `StudioRailItemsSharedSize`) are edited with undo. Inline tool options in the strip are Krita's option widgets found by object name (or by the `"id"` property inside `KisOptionCollectionWidget`, `withId`) and mirrored: selection modes with Grow and Soft (`KisStudioSelectionAdjust`; Soft is a real gaussian, made by three running-sum box blurs on all threads: any width costs the same, where Krita's convolution went through a whole-area FFT), Move's layer choice, Crop's shapes, the fill's threshold, shape fill and outline, gradient shapes. Connections to option widgets need a context that dies with the strip's buttons.
  - Guides (`addGuides`, `plugins/assistants/Assistants`): Krita's assistant types as four kinds (Line, Perspective, Ellipse, Curve), each remembering how it was last used (`[StudioGuides]`). Snap, Lock, Duplicate and Delete are on a guide's right click (`KisAssistantToolMenu.cc`). A brush tool's bar shows a magnet while the image has guides. Captures don't show guides: test through what they change.
- **Picking layers** (`input/kis_select_layer_action`): every click picks, on release; the active top layer passes the click to the next one down; an 8 px drag moves the pressed-on layer.
- **Make brush** (`KisStudioMakeBrush`): the selection's visible pixels become a picture tip (paper color made see-through, cropped, ≤1024 px; gray ones a mask, colored ones a stamp), saved in the brush panel's tag, picked, the quick editor opened on it.
- **Mixing** (`libs/pigment/compositeops/KoCompositeOpMix.h`, `KisStudioMixing`): a brush's paint mixes as Krita does (RGB), evenly (OKLab) or as pigment (a Mixbox-like model fitted in `C:\code\krita-deps\pigments-lab`), the brush setting `SkissaMixing`, chosen as three tiles in the quick editor's Color tab and in the brush sheet. Ops `skissa_mix_{oklab,pigment}_{over,copy}`, converting through the image's profile to linear (`KoCompositeOpInLinear`, keeping channel locks; 8-bit sRGB by tables, only the span a dab's mask covers: LCMS took two thirds of the time); the stroke uses them for Normal (`KisResourcesSnapshot`, smudging's `mixedCompositeOp`). Only the pigment over op is registered, as the layer blend mode Pigment (`KoMixing::layerMode`); brush blend lists leave it out (`KisCompositeOpComboBox::setForBrush`, `isLayerOnly`). Upstream Krita ignores the setting and reads the mode as Normal.
- **OKLCH blend modes** (`compositeops/KoCompositeOpOklch.h`): Hue, Chroma, Color and Lightness in OKLCH (`skissa_oklch_*`, `KoOklch::addTo`), out-of-gamut results giving up chroma. Registered with the mixing ops (`KoCompositeOpMix::all`). Upstream reads them as Normal.
- **Glow blends** (`compositeops/KoCompositeOpGlow.h`): Clip Studio's light modes, where the paint is scaled by its coverage and used as light, so soft, see-through paint glows fully. Add (glow) is Krita's own "Luminosity/Shine (SAI)" renamed in the registry (`KoGlow::addTo`; files stay Krita's); Glow dodge (`skissa_glow_dodge`, `cfGlowDodge`: under / (1 − paint × alpha), the paint as it is where nothing is under it) is added beside the SAI op in `KoCompositeOps.h`. Upstream reads Glow dodge as Normal. A picker's items added after users saved groups go once into their own category's group (`Source::addedLater`, remembered in `<groupsKey>Placed`, so taking one out again sticks).
- **Find** (`KisStudioFind`): one index of keyed items (`tool:…`, `effect:…`, `brush:…`, settings, commands with their classic menu path), ranked by prefix, word start, initials, all-words, then fuzzy, plus frecency and aliases from other apps. Right-click or long-press any button opens its card (`KisStudioItemCard`: shortcut as a re-recordable key cap, variants, pin/remove); a button with a card of its own sets `ownContextMenu`.
- **Settings** (`KisSettingsRegistry` + `KisStudioSettings`):
  - The registry is the single source of truth for every setting: id, default, read/write, flags (`Restart`, `Machine`, `Fork`, `NewViews`), tooltips in `explanations()`. It keeps the history behind settings undo (`KisSettingsUndo`). Write settings through it, not `KConfigGroup`, or they fall out of undo. A write made inside another's `written` hook joins that step; `setValues()` makes several writes one step.
  - `forgetRetired()` is a versioned one-time migration clearing removed settings: bump its version, keep it idempotent. A setting no longer shown must still be read correctly or be migrated; deleting a key upstream still reads is a bug.
  - Frequent non-setting writes (slot brushes, panel places, Find usage) go through `KisSettingsRegistry::writeLater`; a `KisConfig(false)` rewrites the whole kritarc; a raw `KConfigGroup::writeEntry` needs `scheduleSync()` after it or a crash loses it. Krita's own immediate writes stay (its crash detection relies on them).
  - The panel is cards (`Builder`: `toggle`, `number`, `choice`, `color`, `row`…), user sections, Quick, a Changed filter, search and an Undo for changes since it opened; a card's menu is its right click. A setting needing a restart gets a quiet refresh mark and a Restart button; never write "After a restart".
  - `KisSettingsTransfer`: Take Krita's settings / brushes (copies; files here are kept; Krita's files are never written). Export carries the user's arrangement too (`arrangedKeys` prefixes, `arrangedGroups`, kritashortcutsrc); import backs up to `.before-import-<time>`. Add new kinds of arrangement to those lists or export loses them.
- **Theme** (`KisStudioTheme`): a theme is a few params (lightness, tint, tint amount, accent) turned into a generated `.colors` scheme ("Skissa"), plus glass, the glow around the image and the UI font (`held()`). Themes are one list (`[StudioThemes] List` JSON and `Current`, registry settings, so changes are undoable); the shipped ones (Dark, Darker, Night, Moss, Light, Paper, Sky, Blossom) are editable like the user's, with Reset and Restore deleted. Editing a held setting writes into the current theme (`remember`). The picker (`ThemePicker` in `KisStudioSettings.cpp`): dark rows above light, four across; + copies the current one; double-click renames, right-click resets or deletes. First run keeps the look in use (`settle()`, "Mine" if no shipped theme matches); `remake()` renames old placeholder names.
- **Glass** (`KisStudioGlass`, `opengl/KisOpenGLGlass`):
  - Panels over the canvas are painted *by the canvas* (GL blur/refraction behind them); the widget paints only its content. A panel is see-through only after a canvas frame drew all of it (`Panels::drewOver`), solid until then.
  - The look (`glassSource`, after preyneyv's Shadertoy wfGXzh of Apple's Liquid Glass): refraction in a 20 px band at the rim, frost following the glass's thickness (`frostAt`, octave copies mixed with B-spline weights; small textures read with a cubic B-spline, `softAt`), and a one-pixel hairline of concentrated light at the edge (Rim light, `StudioGlassRimLight`). The owner wants the edge thin, sharp and vibrant. Tried and rejected: a mirrored reflection band (big and blurry), light added from the image beside the rim (counted twice), refraction across the whole panel (magnifies, loses the edge), a full rebuild as one refraction-and-frost model (every version worse). Never blend a sharp image straight into a heavily blurred one (a double image). Red and blue taken from the sharp image in the rim band fade in with the bend (`sharp`); taken whole up to the band's inner side, they ended in a visible seam where a strong edge crossed it. There are no refraction or clarity settings.
  - Vibrancy and Grain, both in Oklab and on the CPU glass alike (`vivid()`, `grainAt`, `tinted()`): vibrancy raises chroma by a gain that falls toward sRGB's most vivid chroma (0.32) and fades near black and white (default 50%; 200% reads neon); grain is plain random noise per pixel in Oklab lightness (a sum of three hashes, close to a gaussian), as strong on dark glass as on light, fixed to the panel (default 15%: it also hides banding in wide blurs). Its slight random clumping reads like paper, which the owner likes; a spectrum-shaped (FFT, blue-noise-like) grain faded toward black and white was tried and read as too even and too faint on dark glass. Tried and left out: Text contrast (what is behind taken toward the panel's lightness: at strength it read as a solid panel washed in color, and Opacity already adapts to the brightness behind) and Distortion (bumps shifting what the glass reads, from pixel-sized frost to ripples, lit from the top left): the owner preferred the grain alone, which reads somewhat like paper.
  - Blur goes to 400% (`KisStudioGlass::maxBlur`); past 200% the canvas is downsampled further (`factor` in `KisOpenGLGlass::render`) so the widest kernel stays within its 64 taps.
  - Opacity (default 85%) is one curve each over dark and bright canvas (`tintOverDark`, `tintOverBright`). Each panel's blur is redone only where the canvas redrew (`canvasRedrawn()`).
  - The glow around the image is remade only for changes near its edge; it never falls below a dim neutral (`ambientBacklight`) and bright light is compressed (`ambientKnee`; the owner found 0.5/0.25 overwhelming on a white page, it is 0.4/0.15). Around the image set to black keeps the chosen color apart (`StudioSurroundColor`).
  - Translucent popup windows must never have fully clear pixels (Windows passes the pointer through them and hides the cursor): the style fills them with alpha 1. Captures re-render popups, so they never show first-frame glass bugs.
  - Create framebuffers through `KisOpenGLGlass`'s `texture()`, which keeps the binding: a `QOpenGLFramebufferObject` made mid-frame leaves the wrong one bound and the rest of the frame lands off screen.
  - The shader links on the driver's threads (`Program::finish` checks without waiting; panels paint solid until ready). Waiting froze start for 10+ s. Keep the shader small: every call of a big GLSL function is inlined into the HLSL.
  - Speed: about 0.1 ms a frame at full GPU clock (`KRITA_DEV_FRAMELOG` logs "glass gpu: shrink, blur, draw"). The GPU drops its clock under this light load, so compare timings at equal clocks (`nvidia-smi --query-gpu=clocks.gr -lms 200`) or against a stage left unchanged. The D3D compiler computes every branch of a function chosen per pixel: read each frost level in one place behind its own weight, with `textureLod` (no derivatives). The vertex shader's reads wait one after another: written out a row at a time, they overlap. The mean chroma comes from the blur's weight channel (weight = 1 + salience × chroma), so there is no separate statistics blur.
  - Glass masking skips windows that are ancestors or descendants of each other (a glass panel inside a glass flyout).
- **Effects on the canvas** (`KisStudioEffectOverlay`, `KisFilterManager::setInlineEditor`): any filter previews live on the layer with a small draggable glass panel; Enter keeps, Esc drops, the mask button keeps it editable. It opens with the last applied settings (`KisFilterManager::lastUsedConfiguration`), reset shows while they differ from defaults. Picker tiles render with the same settings at the thumbnail's level of detail, each into its own device. The panel goes in the widest free stretch of canvas. While open, autosave waits (`autosaveHeld`) and a busy image is waited on quietly instead of Krita's modal "Waiting…". A preview may never write the real device outside a `KisTransaction`.
- **Brush editor rows** (`kis_paintop_option`, `kis_paintop_settings_widget`, `kis_categorized_item_delegate`, `plugins/paintops/libpaintop`): each option's row shows its strength (`KisPaintOpOption::RowValue`): drag, double-click to type, right-click for the value from when the brush was picked (`rememberRowValues`); 100% is hidden until hover; the lock is an icon. The Size row is the diameter. "The pen is on" means a sensor is ticked; there is no Enable Pen Settings checkbox (`rowCheckedLens`, `sensorsLens`). Opacity and Flow get a row checkbox for the pen; MyPaint rows don't (MyPaint drops input curves saved with the pen off). Saved curves (`KisCurvePresetStrip`) are global in `[SavedCurves]`.
- **Quick brush editor** (`KisStudioBrushEditor`, `KisStudioPenPopup`, `plugins/paintops/libpaintop/KisBrushTipEditor`):
  - Opens from the brush chip on the top bar, its action, or a second click on the active Paint/Blend/Erase slot. A test stroke, the brush's parts as tabs (Tip, Mask, Texture, Color; then Stroke), each with pictures and its settings as rows; "+ Add" shows the rest as tiles of dabs (`widgets/KisStudioDabPictures`). Choices are pictures (`KisStudioChips::setTileColumns`).
  - It edits the paintop box's live option widgets (`KisPaintopBox::currentOptionWidget()`), taking a use of them (`KisPaintopBox::useOptionWidget`); each user takes and gives back its own use.
  - An option shows itself through `KisPaintOpOption`: `rowValue()`, `penInputs()`/`setPenInputActive`/`setPenCurve`/`penMode`, `createPenInputPage`, `hasQuickPage`/`createQuickPage(category)`.
  - The tip editor draws the real dab (`KisAutoBrushFactory`) with handles for size, softness (`fadeSpot`) and angle; under it the edge (`EdgeCanvas`): softness as one measure for every shape (`hardnessOf`/`setHardness`) and three shapes, Round, Smooth and Curve (`heldAt`); pressing the line makes it a curve. Handles are white with a dark ring and lines dark under light, so nothing disappears on any dab. Krita stores a taller tip turned a quarter. Angle selectors have no flip buttons.
  - Size and Spacing rows are the tip's (`KisBrushBasedPaintopOptionWidget::paintOpOptionAdded`); Spacing has its Auto on the row (`setAutomatic`, `RowValue::automatic`). A switch belongs on the row of the value it frees.
  - The brush sheet (`KisStudioBrushSheet`): a column per part with every setting, options without a row as "Name ›" chips showing Krita's own page (`lendPage`/`returnPage`, except `quickPageIsWhole`), the tip page with all of Krita's Brush Tip page (`KisBrushTipQuickPage::updateRest`), a Blend mode row, a pen grid (settings × inputs, `[StudioBrushSheet] PenColumns`; click on, click again closes the curve, right click off), Save, Save as new, other engines, Copy as a picture. Krita's editor is behind its ⋯. Mask modes are pictures made with Krita's masking ops; blend modes a stroke over the image (`KisBlendModePicker::modePicture`). Random color sources show only for brushes that use them. Columns ask for their height at their width (`Column`): `adjustSize()` leaves out wrapping chip rows.
  - Re-stroke (`KisLastStrokeReplay`): while an editor is open, a brush change repaints the last stroke of that brush (recorded by the freehand helper; its step replaced with `setIndex`, not `undo()`, which takes settings back first), only while that step is newest. Smoothing changes replay from the pen's own path. When testing settings by painting, paint a throwaway dot before each switch.
  - A row that is an optional effect (Rotation, Scatter) comes off by its cross or pulled up or down off the editor; pulled onto the top bar it is pinned there. Sideways is always the value.
- **Smoothing** (`KisStudioSmoothing`): the brush tool's smoothing as one amount, shown as a fill behind the brush name on the tool chip: drag changes it, right click resets, click opens the editor. Per brush in `[StudioBrushSmoothing]` by file. The stabilizer evens pressure and tilt over ~25 ms only (`stabilizedSensorsReach`; Krita's whole window flattened quick strokes). Held W and a sideways drag change it too (`KisDragValueAction`).
- **Stroke tab** (`KisStudioStrokePage`): the editor's last tab for freehand tools: a wobbly line run through Krita's weighted and stabilizer math, kinds as pills (Off is Krita's Basic), Strength (or slow/fast apart), Follows zoom, Smooth Pressure, Pixel perfect for pixel brushes, Shapes, snapping to guides. Dyna and Multibrush get Tool options for their own.
- **Brush tweaks across a restart** (`KisStudioBrushTweaks`): with Keep brush tweaks on, dirty brushes' settings go to `brush-tweaks.json` in the profile folder (keyed by file and md5; only brushes changed since are serialized, the file written on a thread of its own) and come back at the first window unless the file changed. Brush files are never written. Pick a brush through `KisPaintopBox::resourceSelected`, as Krita's panel does: `setPaintOpPreset` alone leaves the settings widget on the old engine.
- **Pinned controls** (`KisStudioControls`, `KisStudioControlsPanel`): a brush setting (`brushsetting:<option id>`) or a Krita setting (`setting:<registry id>`) as a row on the top bar or in the Controls panel; pins show on hover, rows are pulled to move or remove them. Fractions show as percent.
- **Fills** (`libs/image/floodfill/KisLineArtFill`, `KisColorDrop`, `plugins/tools/basictools/kis_tool_fill.cc`):
  - `KisLineArtFill::grow` runs after Krita's flood fill (`KisFillPainter::setLineArt`): a watershed so the fill reaches under the lines to where they meet the next area, taking in soft edges and specks. A fill over 24 MP grows by tiles of 4096 px, each reading 128 px around it from the fill as the flood made it. The Fill tool has Under lines and Specks. Layer → Auto Flats colors every enclosed area (`KisLineArtFill::flats`).
  - Every fill (a drop, the Fill tool, Enclose and Fill) chooses its layer alike (`KisColorDrop::fillTarget`): on line art a "Colors" layer (below clear line art, Multiply above opaque). A gap of 0 is Auto, sized from the line width (`autoGap`). What counts as a line is dark ink, not earlier fills (`isColorsLayer`).
  - A color dragged onto the canvas (rail chip, swatches, palette) previews the area under the pointer; after the drop a panel by it sets the gap, Spread and Under lines (refilling) and keeps filling (`KeepFillingChip`). With a modifier held, Krita's own color drop runs. A color dropped on a layer row recolors it (`KisNodeModel::dropMimeData`).
  - A fill through `FillProcessingVisitor` needs a `KisUpdateCommand` with its dirty rect in the same stroke or the canvas doesn't update.
- **Shapes** (`tool/KisStrokeShape`, `KisToolFreehand`): with Shapes on, a stroke held still half a second at its end becomes a line, arc, ellipse/circle, polygon/rectangle or polyline, as one undo step (`repaintAs`, keeping pressure). Thresholds are in screen pixels, tuned so scribbles, waves and spirals never snap. The colorize mask brush opts out (`m_snapsToShapes`).
- **Time-lapse** (`plugins/dockers/skissa_recorder`): a scrubbable preview, record, clip length, MP4/GIF/WebM chips, Export beside the image; the rest in ⋯. The frames folder (thousands after a long session) is listed off the window's thread. Krita's recorder and ffmpeg underneath.
- **Symmetry** (`KisStudioSymmetry`, `plugins/tools/basictools/KisStudioMultiBrush` in place of `kis_tool_multihand`): Mirror/Radial/Tile; Radial and Tile use Multibrush and return to the previous tool. Handles via `paintToolOutline`.
- **Window frame** (`KisStudioWindowFrame`): the window draws its own title bar. Maximized it fills the work area asked from Windows (Qt's state lags), less 2 px on an auto-hiding taskbar's edge. Window animations are off in canvas-only mode.
- **Its own folders** (`libs/global/KisProfile`): nothing is shared with a regular Krita. Config and logs in `<LocalAppData>/<name>/` (`KisProfile::configFile`), resources in `<AppData>/<name>/krita`, autosaves in `<Temp>/<name>`; its own single-instance key. Every new file goes through `KisProfile`, never a bare `QStandardPaths::GenericConfigLocation` or `"kritarc"`. The dev profile keeps the old layout in `qttest`; `KRITA_DEV_APART=1` runs the new layout there.
- **Name and links** (`KisProfile::name()`, `KisProfile::homepage()`): text meaning this app says Skissa; text about Krita the project, its files or docs stays Krita. Report Bug opens the homepage's issues. Krita's updater is off (`ENABLE_UPDATERS`).
- **Saving on Windows** (`KisSafeSave`, from `KisImportExportManager`): write beside, flush, swap with `ReplaceFileW` (keeps permissions, streams, cloud history), so a failed save never breaks the file; formats that write their own file (`supportsIO()` false) go through `KisSafeSave::besides` and `replaceWith`. A .kra's entries after the mimetype are Zip64 (`KoQuaZipStore`).
- **Releases** (`packaging/windows/make-release.py`): a portable zip following the DLL imports, with license texts and SOURCE.txt for the exact tag; DLLs loaded by name go in `LOADED`. Build from `env\_install` (read only) into `krita-deps\release` while no install runs. It is built with `HIDE_SAFE_ASSERTS` off, so `kis_assert.cpp` shows the "internal error" box only in the dev profile.
- **Updates** (`KisSkissaUpdater`): only a release folder (with `skissa-files.txt`) checks GitHub 30 s after start and every 4 hours (`StudioUpdates`), downloads the zip into the profile's `updates` folder (resuming, sha256 checked), unpacks it as `<folder>.update`; on quit `update.ps1` (logging `update.log`) swaps folders by renames, keeping `<folder>.old` (removed by the new version two minutes after it starts, unless it holds the user's files: `removeReplacedInstall`), or moves the contents when the folder can't be renamed (`Move-Contents`). The script that runs is the old version's, so its fixes take effect one update later. A start while a downloaded update waits (in the seconds between quitting and the swap, or after a swap the folder held up) hands it to the swap and ends, the swap then starting the new version (`startWaitingUpdate`, from `main.cc`; not with files to open or Skissa already open); the script takes a mutex (`Local\SkissaUpdate`), so a second one waits and only starts the program, and after a failed swap it starts the version in place with `SKISSA_NO_SWAP` set, so the start doesn't hand off again. Seen on the owner's PC: Skissa opened again two seconds after quitting ran the old version, held the folder, and its own quit started a second swap. Helpers must start elsewhere than the program folder (`setWorkingDirectory`): Windows won't rename a folder a process works in. Restart goes through it (`KisSkissaUpdater::restart`). Version: `packaging/skissa-version.txt` (`SKISSA_VERSION`). Test with `KRITA_DEV_UPDATES=<url>` (a JSON like GitHub's; the zip served over HTTP, e.g. `python -m http.server`, since a file:// download has no status and is dropped) on a release folder run with `KRITA_DEV_PROFILE=1` and `KRITA_DEV_APART=1`, started from its bin folder.
- **Publishing** (`devtools\release.ps1`, `packaging/skissa-release.py`): from a clean `main`: next patch version (or `-Version`), committed, built, zipped, tagged, pushed, GitHub release (`-NotesFile`, else commit subjects; `-NoPublish` stops at the zip). Only when the owner asks.
- **Translations** (`packaging/skissa-translations.py`): the fork's text gets only translations Krita already has, written to `po-skissa/<lang>.po`; Krita's `po/` stays upstream's, joined at build (`cmake/modules/SkissaTranslations.cmake`). `OTHER_SENSE` lists text used in another sense. Never write translations by hand; rerun after merging upstream or changing text.
- **What Studio leaves out**: classic-only menu items are hidden while Studio is on (`hiddenToolbarActions`) and Find skips hidden actions. Panel plugins the Studio covers and the G'MIC bridge aren't built (`plugins/dockers/CMakeLists.txt`; sources stay for merges); the toolbox is only hidden (`KisStudioPanelPicker::isRetired`). After dropping a plugin, delete its DLL from the install: `cmake --install` never removes files.
- **Small systems:**
  - Studio popups (`widgets/KisStudioPopup`): a glass panel in its own window, closed by a press elsewhere unless pinned; popups opened from it count as inside (`KisPopupCloser`). A plain `QWidget` (no `Q_OBJECT`, no mouse tracking) counts as background that drags the window (`KisStudioGlass::setMovableByBackground`): give interactive widgets their own class. Popups and dragged dialogs stay on screen (`DialogMover::onScreen`).
  - Toggling: a press on a popup's anchor closes it and is swallowed, so buttons toggle their popups (`KisPopupCloser::installToggle`, for every popup and menu; `popupBeside` passes the anchor). A press within 150 ms after one closed it, and Qt's double click, are swallowed too. Only closing by the anchor arms this (arming on every close swallowed fast clicks between buttons); input-less windows (a floating message) aren't popups. Buttons need no guards of their own. The owner finds a second click that reopens a popup obviously wrong.
  - An icon-only button whose press opens its menu (⋯) has no menu arrow (`menuArrowLeftOut`).
  - Empty lists say why they're empty (`KisStudioEmptyStates`).
  - Layer rows (`plugins/dockers/skissa_layers`: `LayerBox`, `NodeDelegate`): a sideways drag is the opacity (no slider); blend mode and opacity show only when unusual, on hover for the current row, always in touch mode (`updateRowShown`); double-click types, right-click makes it full (`opacityRect`). Let go at 0%, the layer is hidden instead, keeping its opacity, as one undo step.
  - Shortcuts have places (`KisStudioShortcuts::toolsOf`/`shareContext`): tool actions work only while their tools are in use; only keys in the same place clash, and the Keys page (`dialogs/KisKeysPage`, saving 300 ms after an edit, `storeEdited`) marks only those. Lists of actions joined from several places must be made unique, by name too.
  - The color panel's picker (`ColorPicker` in `KisStudioColorPanel.cpp`) is two choices in its style menu: hues around a Ring (clockwise from red at the top) or down a Strip beside the shape (`StudioColorHues`), and the shape: HSV's Triangle (Krita's upright one) or Square, or OKLCH (`StudioColorShape`). Sliders, a strip across and a diamond were tried in a demo and left out, as were other hue spacings (OKLCH, vivid OKLCH, a painter's RYB wheel): HSV spacing it is. In the HSV shapes, turning the hue keeps the color's OKLCH lightness (`StudioColorKeepLightness`, on by default, `holdLightness`): at the saturation last picked, or with less where full value is too dark, which comes back with the hue. OKLCH lightness, not luminance, so it matches the value view. The OKLCH shape keeps lightness by itself.
  - The OKLCH picker's ring and the hue slider are spaced as an HSV wheel (`oklchHueOfHsv`/`hsvHueOfOklch`, one monotonic table `ringHues` in `KisStudioColorPanel.cpp`); the owner prefers that to OKLCH spacing. The hex box takes any color format typed (`readColor`) and shows hex; Copy uses the last chosen format (`StudioColorCopyFormat`); a multi-format field was tried and the owner prefers the plain box. In float images an intensity chip sets stops past white (`IntensityChip`, `brightened`, `StudioColorIntensity`).
  - Pickers (`widgets/KisStudioPicker`: effects, brushes, blend modes, tips, patterns, panels, settings) show the user's own first, everything else below in one list; no "All N"/"Only yours" link (the owner dislikes two-click reveals). Groups work as tags, stored per picker (`Source::groupsKey`, e.g. `StudioBlendModeGroups` JSON with `…Yours` and `…Version`): Krita's categories start as groups (`initialGroups`, `initialGroupsOf`), the rest under Other; right-click toggles groups, titles rename in place and drag; empty groups go. The owner wants no ready-made groups beyond Krita's categories.
  - Zoom steps are even by ratio, each caught by a simple ratio (`KoZoomMode::generateStandardZoomLevels`): 100, 125, 150, 200…, 80, 67, 50… (`TestKoZoomAction` fails by design).
  - Tooltips (`KisStudioTips`): the name, then each gesture as a little mouse picture and a word (`KisStudioTips::html`; written in code as "Drag: change\nRight-click: reset"), never colon-and-line text blocks. Sized to their text up to 380 px (`sized`), placed beside small controls (`placeBeside`), hidden when they only repeat the button's words, all off with `StudioTooltips`. Jargon choices carry one-line tips (`KisStudioPenPopup::inputTip`, `KisStudioBrushEditor::optionTip`). The brush tooltip is a card (`resourcewidgets/KisIconToolTip`).
  - Touch mode (`KisTouchMode`) shows what otherwise appears only on hover; a finger held still after a drag isn't a reset; menus open on the context menu event. A long press arrives as a plain right click: `longPressAt` on `qApp` tells them apart.
  - Brushes as strokes (`widgets/KisPresetStrokeThumbnails`, `kis_preset_chooser.cpp`): each brush paints a wave, after its picture (`PresetStrokePictures`); a brush that paints nothing there (blenders) is repainted with full color rate and no smudging, fading out. With Names on strokes off (`PresetStrokeNames`) a row is its stroke alone, centered. Strokes flow into as many columns as fit comfortably (`KisPresetChooser::compactStrokeRows`, a grid set on the view itself, a little short of the whole width or Qt wraps a row that fills it exactly). The brush picker (`KisStudioBrushPicker`) follows the same setting (pictures when off); its strokes are kept one a brush, and those all of the paint color are recolored when the color changes instead of painted again. Backdrops to smear were tried; the owner found them loaded and hard to read.
  - Hangs of 10 s log the main thread's stack to `krita-hang.log` in the profile folder (`krita/KisHangWatch`); a hang leaves nothing in the crash log. `KRITA_DEV_STALLS=<ms>` logs shorter stalls to `krita-stalls.log`. Stack walking: `libs/global/KisStackWalk`.
  - Autosave waits for a pause in work, at most a minute (`KisDocument`). Per-layer stroke logs are saved in .kra (`image/KisStrokeLog`). Krita reopens the last image at start, guarded against crash loops.
  - Frame pacing for VRR screens (`KisUpdatePacer`); on ANGLE Qt composes after a flush, not a finish (`composeWithoutWaiting`).
  - Krita keeps a tool manager per window: reach it through `KisStudioTools` and connect each part to its own window's.
  - Overlays stay on top with `KisStudioLayout::keepAbove`; raise only what is being dragged (two overlays raising themselves swap and repaint forever).
  - Shared helpers, one copy each: sRGB and Oklab (`KisColorMath.h`), color mix (`KisModernStyle::mix`), search scores (`KisStudioPicker::matchScore`), popup shadow room (`KisStudioGlass::shadowMargin`). Look for one before writing another.

## Dev workflow (Windows)

The build environment is outside the repo, in `C:\code\krita-deps`: llvm-mingw, KDE's prebuilt Qt5 deps in `env\_install`, the build dir `build`, helper scripts in `devtools`, and in its root `run-krita.bat`, `profile.py`, `baseline-tests.bat` and `known-failing-tests.txt`.

- **The fork's own files in `libs/ui` are listed in `libs/ui/skissa.cmake`**, not kritaui's list (they rebuild in ~5 s instead of ~14 s). Add new `libs/ui` files there; form headers (`ui_*.h`) they include go in `kritaui_skissa_forms`.
- **One command:** `devtools\dev.ps1` builds, closes the dev Krita and waits, installs and launches (~10–20 s). Flags: `-NoBuild`, `-NoLaunch`. Build errors are matched case-sensitively; cmake's Python `ModuleNotFoundError` lines are harmless. After a build, check the installed DLL's time: an install can keep the old one if the dev Krita was still running. Two sessions building in `build` at once break CMake's regeneration: build again.
- **Always run the dev build through `run-krita.bat`** / `launchdev.ps1` (hidden console). It sets `KRITA_DEV_PROFILE=1`, so settings and resources live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\qttest` and `%APPDATA%\qttest`. **Never touch the owner's real Krita profile** (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\kritarc`, `%APPDATA%\krita`).
- **Driving the app:** the dev build listens for posted window messages (`krita/KisDevHooks.cpp`): `KritaDevCapture` (renders its own windows to a PNG; no screen capture, ever), `KritaDevMouse`, `KritaDevKey`, `KritaDevTree`, `KritaDevExport`, `KritaDevShortcuts`, `KritaDevColorDrop`. Helpers in `devtools`: `devinput.ps1` (move, click, rclick, line, drag, type, key, capture), `pen.ps1` (a pressure stroke), `colordrop.ps1`, `tree.ps1`, `kritawin.ps1`.
  - Captures miss QPainter-only canvas decorations (guides); GL-drawn tool outlines show.
  - Synthetic moves don't move the OS cursor: use the event's `globalPos`, not `QCursor::pos()`. They do hover item views (with `WA_Hover` on the viewport).
  - Synthetic presses go to the topmost window, a tooltip too: move and press without waiting. Capture before clicking by coordinates; the owner may have moved the bar.
  - Synthetic drags don't move the tip editor's chips or Grow/Soft scrubbers: test those through what they change.
  - In popups, `QContextMenuEvent::pos()` can be in window coordinates: map from `globalPos()`.
  - In a fresh profile (`KRITA_DEV_APART`) the UI scale differs, so devinput coordinates are logical pixels.
- **The shell sees a stale copy of `%LOCALAPPDATA%`** (the desktop app is an MSIX package). To read or edit the real dev `kritarc`, copy it with a WMI-launched process (`Invoke-CimMethod Win32_Process Create`), and edit it only while the dev Krita is closed. Console programs started through WMI hang when Windows Terminal is the default console: start them detached (`CreateFlags = 8`, `ShowWindow = 0`).
- **Back up the dev profile right before each test that writes to it,** and restore exactly what was there; the owner works in the dev build between turns. Prefer the UI (with the panel's Undo) over editing files. Ctrl+Z in a test also undoes settings toggles.
- **It's fine to close the dev Krita** (unsaved changes and all) to rebuild. Only kill processes whose path is under `C:\code\krita-deps` (the owner's installed Krita may be running; scripts look for `skissa` and `krita`). After a kill, delete `.sunset.png-autosave.kra`, or the next start waits on recovery.
- Another session may be driving the dev Krita (maybe with other variables): a fresh-looking profile in a capture may be that, not the code.
- **README clips:** `devtools\rec.py` drives the app and captures frames (stop-motion, ~1 s a capture); save as animated WebP.
- **Startup profiling:** `KRITA_DEV_SAMPLE=<file>`, then `profile.py <file> --timeline 250` (`--callers`/`--callees`). `QT_LOGGING_RULES=krita.performance.debug=true` prints Krita's phase timings. Most start time is Krita's. `KRITA_DEV_FRAMELOG` gives each frame's cost and paints.

Run it with `KRITA_DEV_APART=1` after closing the dev Krita (a second one hands itself over), and not while tests build. It picks what to press by text, tooltip, object or accessible name: unnamed buttons may get Close pressed. Every early return in a step must `end()` it. Its photos have artifacts (popup order, pointer-placed pickers, transparent glass dialogs): check findings by hand.

## Updating from Krita

`origin` is Krita (KDE), `skissa` is the fork's own repo. Merge Krita in often (small merges are easy); never rebase the branch.

- **One step:** `devtools\update.ps1` runs `packaging/skissa-update.py`: fetch, merge (rerere on), translations refreshed and committed, build, install, start and capture. Exit 2 leaves the merge open: settle, commit, run it again. Check the capture, then push.
- **Check a merge first:** `git merge-tree --write-tree --name-only HEAD origin/master` lists the files that would conflict.
- **Keep Krita's files mergeable:** new code in new files called with a line or two; turn Krita's code off in CMake rather than deleting it; don't append to Krita's lists when a fork file can hold it (`po-skissa/`, `libs/ui/skissa.cmake`).
- **Skissa's own copies of Krita's files**, built in their place: the layer, palette and time-lapse panels (`plugins/dockers/skissa_layers`, `skissa_palette`, `skissa_recorder`) and Multibrush (`KisStudioMultiBrush`). Krita's fixes to those don't merge: the update script lists Krita's commits that touched them (`KEPT` in `skissa-update.py`) to bring over by hand.
- **Where the fork still changes Krita's code most** (candidates to move behind a call): `KisMainWindow.cpp`, `kis_tool_freehand_helper.cpp`, `plugins/paintops/libpaintop` option widgets, `kis_preset_chooser.cpp`, `kis_paintop_settings_widget.cpp`, `KisOpenGLCanvasRenderer.cpp`, `kis_config.cc`, `kis_slider_spin_box_p.h`, `kis_cmb_composite.cc`, `kis_paintop_box.cc`, `KisTagChooserWidget.cpp`. ## Lessons (append, newest last)

- Check the dev profile's state before blaming code: a "wrong" value is often a setting.
- Count only deliberate input as "the user is working": pen hover sends moves all the time.
- Public text (README, release notes) is shown, not told: a clip or screenshot per feature, a line of plain first-person words at most. Release notes are pictures and a line or two of facts. Selling, bold lead-ins, feature lists and comparison tables read as marketing or machine-written to the owner.
- Windows caches program icons by path: after the .exe's icon changes, `ie4uinit.exe -show` refreshes it.
- Python one-liners in a bash heredoc turn `\n` inside C++ strings into real newlines: make escape-heavy edits with the Edit tool.
- PowerShell's `[int]` rounds to even: use `[math]::Floor` in test scripts. `Set-Content -Encoding utf8` writes a BOM, which CMake files can't take. `Copy-Item` keeps the old modified time, so ninja won't rebuild a copied-back source: touch it.
- Widgets made before `KisStudioGlass::installMenuGlass()` were polished without it: anything new must be polished before its native window is created.
- A stroke's step reaches the undo stack a moment after the image goes idle: wait a turn of the event loop before deciding a stroke was cancelled.
- A drag owned by the widget it starts from dies with it: parent drags to `qApp` and delete them after `exec()`.
- A drag must check `event->buttons()` on every move: a release in another window never arrives.
- A tool's `updateSettingsViews()` must block its widgets' signals while setting them, or stale neighbours get written back.
- Windows headers define `near` and `far` as macros: don't use them as names.
- A widget's Leave event also arrives while its children are being deleted: hover handlers touching children must not run then (`HoverWatch` defers).
- Krita's stroke preview (`KisPresetLivePreviewView`) paints over its palette's window color: give it a transparent Window and Base to show it on glass.
- A lambda taking a `const QString &` into a list must not refresh that list before it is done: take such ids by value.
- Classes without `Q_OBJECT` (`KisStudioPanelColumn`) can't be found with `findChildren<T *>()`, which returns every widget: use `dynamic_cast` (`columnsBeside()`).
- A column sized by hand needs `QLayout::SetNoConstraint`, or its layout keeps an old minimum size.
- `setParent` hides a widget: read its visibility first and restore it.
- A view in fit mode refits whenever panel insets change: the canvas controller refits only while panels settle in (2 s from the first insets).
- A panel shown inside a hidden group gets no Show event: the stack watches ShowToParent and HideToParent (`shownChanged`).
- A fixed-height widget with no layout has no size hint (-1): read `minimumHeight()`.
- A hidden QDockWidget title bar still keeps its room: give it no height (`PanelGrip::setAlone`).
- A widget's drag filter takes events only from its own children, or presses passed through from elsewhere start drags.
- Blend ops must keep channels switched off (channel locks) and clear them where nothing was painted (`KoRgbU8ColorSpaceTester`).
- Writing a layer's or the selection's device outside a `KisTransaction` while under undo corrupts the history (`KisMementoManager::getMemento`).
- With `-fmath-errno`, `std::sqrt` in a loop doesn't vectorize; `std::sqrt(std::fmax(x, 0.0f))` does.
- `QWidget::mapTo` crashes unless the target is an ancestor: map through `mapToGlobal`/`mapFromGlobal`.
- Segoe UI Variable has no italic: `KisModernStyle::polish` gives italic text Segoe UI (`italicFace`); italic set after polishing isn't caught.
- An item view's hover look needs `WA_Hover` on its viewport (`KisResourceItemListView`).
- Krita's slider boxes (`kis_slider_spin_box_p.h`) veil themselves while typing; ending the typing must redraw what's under them. Check fields after typing, clicking again and clicking away.
- Anything moving over the canvas repaints every panel each frame: keep row painting cheap (a stroked `QPainterPath` halo on brush names cost 22 ms a frame).
- A group's place is a share of the room `spanY` gives it: anything that writes a place must use that same room.
- Caches of pictures need a bound by bytes, not only count; kept files stay small (logs restart past 1 MB, stale entries dropped).
- Measure before optimizing: the composite ops' cost was the color conversion, not the mixing math. A benchmark from `build-tests` (`KoCompositeOpsBenchmark`) loads color plugins from whatever folders it finds, the installed Skissa too: copy the .exe into `env\_install\bin` to run it against the dev install, with `QT_FORCE_STDERR_LOGGING=1` to see its output.
