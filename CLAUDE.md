# Skissa: how we build it

This is the working guide for this fork. Read it before changing anything; it explains what we value, how the main systems fit together and how to work with the owner. **It is a living document: when you learn something that a future agent needs (a principle the owner states, a mistake worth not repeating, a system you added), add it here in the right section, briefly, and remove what has become false.**

## What this is

Skissa (Swedish: "to sketch") is a personal fork of [Krita](https://invent.kde.org/graphics/krita) (upstream: KDE). Branch `main`, pushed to the `skissa` remote's `main`. The owner is a digital artist who paints in Krita daily and wants it to feel as obvious and visual as the tablet apps (Procreate-like) without losing Krita's power. The fork rebuilds the interface ("Studio"); the painting engine, file formats and resources stay Krita's.

The owner works fast and reviews results afterwards. They mostly talk about how things look and feel, not about code.

## How to work with the owner

- **Automate; don't ask what you can decide.** Build, install, relaunch, test and commit without checking in. Ask only for decisions that are really theirs (design direction they reserved, outward-facing actions like pushing or publishing).
- **"Give me suggestions" means text only.** Don't implement suggestions until they say "implement", "do it", or pick numbers. When they pick, do exactly those.
- **Finish every task in a message**, including ones sent mid-turn. Keep a list.
- **Verify visually.** Launch the dev build and capture the window (see Dev workflow) before saying a UI change works. Say plainly what you could not verify.
- **Report like a person:** what changed, what they'll notice, what's left. Short.
- When the owner says something feels wrong, believe it and find the cause; don't argue from the code.
- Before changing a look the owner has opinions on (glass, glow), get a reference image of what they want; don't iterate on numbers blind.

## Design principles

These come from the owner's feedback over many sessions. They decide most questions.

1. **The canvas is the product.** Chrome is a cost. Everything else appears where and when it's needed and gets out of the way.
2. **Needing explanation is a design problem.** Before adding a hint or tooltip, make the control self-evident: a picture, a live preview, direct manipulation, a better default, or removing the option.
3. **No grey helper text. Ever.** No info icons, no descriptions under settings, no "click to…" labels. A short tooltip is the fallback. Grey (secondary ink) is for quiet *values or state*, never for instructions.
4. **Sentence case.** "Open recent", not "Open Recent". Krita's Title Case text is shown in sentence case in English (`KisSentenceCase`; names and acronyms keep their capitals; translations untouched): menu commands (`KisStudioLayout::tidyCommand`, which also keeps classic-only items hidden), form captions without their colon (`KisModernStyle::polish`), option names (`KisPaintOpOption::label`), widget tooltips (`KisStudioTips`). Items the user named (files, layers, brushes) never go through it.
5. **Shortest clear text.** "Layer 5", not "Paint Layer 5". Drop type names, restated context and "Click to". Most controls need no tooltip: if the name says it, leave it out. When one is needed, it's one line an artist would say, telling what the name can't ("Huge brushes paint slowly", "Nearest is best for pixel art"): no restating the name, no "X, but Y" or "…, so …", no "e.g.". A short name before ": " shows in bold above the rest (`leadOf`). A button whose name says what it does gets just the name.
6. **Direct manipulation over forms.** Drag a value on its own label or row. Handles on the canvas instead of number fields. Typing is a double-click away, in place, no box (`widgets/KisStudioInlineEdit`). **Right-click resets a value to its default.** Shift drags finer. The wheel over a value changes it too (custom ones: `KisModernStyle::setWheelValue`); a turn of the wheel belongs to the widget it started over (`WheelLatch`), so a page scrolled past sliders keeps scrolling and never changes them.
7. **Show, don't name.** Live previews on the real image (effects on canvas, blend modes as thumbnails of the actual picture, brush tooltips with strokes), curves as little drawings, themes as swatches.
8. **Fewer options, better defaults.** Remove settings nobody should need. Merge duplicates. Put an option where its subject is (the eraser's own size on the Size page) and also in Settings so it can be found.
9. **Organized by intent, found by search.** Group by what the artist wants ("Blur", "Darken"), not by implementation. Everything is reachable with Find (Ctrl+K), including words from other apps.
10. **Perceptual controls.** Brush size moves by ratios (log); opacity stays linear.
11. **Stable places, personal content.** Primary controls don't move. Customizing uses the same gestures as using (drag, right-click) and always has a reset.
12. **One undo.** Ctrl+Z also undoes settings changes (and rail and bar changes), interleaved with document steps by time.
13. **Made for people, not for convention.** A choice needs a reason in how people see, move, remember and change their minds, or in the physics being imitated; "other apps do it" isn't one, and neither is a rule kept for its own sake. Hands aim roughly, people decide as they go, recognize faster than they recall and learn by repeating: so input may be approximate, anything can be changed after it is done, nothing is asked that the program can answer, and one continuous control beats a set of modes.
14. **Stay Krita underneath.** Files and presets the fork writes must open in upstream Krita and the other way round. Never change a preset just by loading it. Keep upstream code paths intact so merges stay easy.

## Look and feel

All tokens live in `libs/widgetutils/KisModernStyle.h`. Use them; don't invent numbers.

- **Spacing:** `hairline` 2, `space1` 4, `space2` 8, `space3` 12, `space4` 16. Nothing else.
- **Corners:** squircles everywhere (`KisModernStyle::squircle`). Radii: `cornerRadius` 5 (controls), `tileRadius` 8, `cardRadius` 10, `panelRadius` 14. Checkboxes, swatches, switches and segmented controls are squircles (`fullSquircleRadius` for checkboxes). Radio buttons stay circles, and so do the paint colors on the rail (the color chip and colors pinned beside it): a drop of paint, the owner's call.
- **Motion:** one curve, an exponential ease-out (`motionCurve()`), and three durations: `enterDuration` 180 ms, `exitDuration` 120 ms, `changeDuration` 160 ms. Things that move glide, they don't jump.
- **Hover after scrolling:** a wheel turn tells the window the pointer moved where it rests (`WheelLatch::rehoverSoon`), so what scrolled under it lights and what scrolled away doesn't.
- **Fields:** a framed combo box is drawn as a field too (an opaque button fill read as old and pasted on). A text field is a soft fill of the text's color at low alpha with `tileRadius` corners, as value rows are (an opaque Button fill read as a pasted-on box on glass); focused, a little more of it with a soft edge, never a bright accent ring.
- **Ink:** text in the palette's text color; quiet values in `secondaryInk()`; fills and tracks as text color at low alpha (works on any theme and on glass). The accent is optional (themes can have none).
- **Selected rows:** use `KisModernStyle::selectedRow()` / `selectedRowInk()`, never a mix toward `QPalette::Highlight`: a theme without an accent has a gray Highlight made for checks and slider fills (dark on light themes), which made selected rows a heavy mid-gray with white text.
- **Glass:** panels, popups and dialogs are frosted glass over the canvas. See Glass below before touching any panel painting.
- **Icons:** `krita/pics/svg/{dark,light}_studio_*.svg`, generated by `krita/pics/svg/make_studio_icons.py` (24 px grid, round strokes; a switch that is on is drawn solid). The app icon is a bare pencil S (white with a soft shadow on dark, ink on light; Krita names icons by their own color, so `dark_krita-branding` is the ink one). The window icon follows Windows' light/dark setting live (`taskbarIcon()` in `KisApplication`); the .exe gets the white S with a stronger shadow at small sizes. Every icon file (file icons, macOS, Android, Windows Store tiles) comes from `krita/pics/branding/make_icon.py`; the splash wordmark is `krita/data/splash/banner.svg`.

- [`docs/skissa/small-systems.md`](docs/skissa/small-systems.md): Color fields, popups and toggling, layer rows, shortcuts, color picker, pickers, value steps and drag law, value view, image drops, clip, Push, effect brush, rail colors, input tokens, zoom steps, tooltips, touch mode, brush strokes thumbnails, hang logs, autosave, frame pacing, focus/keys, overlays, shared helpers.
- **Tools** (`KisStudioToolRail`, `KisStudioToolStrip`): 33 tools in 9 slots (Paint, Blend, Erase, Select, Transform, Fill, Shape, Text, Guides; Blend and Erase are the brush tool too, `toolSlots()`). Slots remember their variant; the brush in use decides whether Paint or Blend is lit. The rail's items (`StudioRailItems`: slots, single tools, spaces, Effects, and brushes pinned by dragging them from a brush list, which paint with their own size unless in `StudioRailItemsSharedSize`) are edited with undo. Inline tool options in the strip are Krita's option widgets found by object name (or by the `"id"` property inside `KisOptionCollectionWidget`, `withId`) and mirrored: selection modes with Grow and Soft (`KisStudioSelectionAdjust`; Soft is a real gaussian, made by three running-sum box blurs on all threads: any width costs the same, where Krita's convolution went through a whole-area FFT; up to 3 px its own small kernel, since three boxes come out the same there), Move's layer choice, Crop's shapes, the fill's threshold, shape fill and outline, gradient shapes, the color sampler's source and size, the magic wand's and similar color's threshold (a drag makes the last click's selection again as it goes: `KisRedoLast` takes back that click's step with `setIndex` while it is the last one done and runs the click again, one at a time; the Fill tool's settings and the color drop's panel redo the last fill the same way), the text tool's font (written in itself, opening `KisStudioFontPicker`, set through the canvas's text property data). Connections to option widgets need a context that dies with the strip's buttons. What the bar mirrors is hidden in the tool's options panel (`tuck`, `tuckSection`, kept hidden when the tool shows it again); with nothing left there the chip opens the bar's ⋯ instead (the text tool's QML page, comic panels, reference images with none picked). The text tool's ⋯ drives the object its QML page uses (`studioTextOptions` property).
  - Guides (`addGuides`, `plugins/assistants/Assistants`): Krita's assistant types as four kinds (Line, Perspective, Ellipse, Curve), each remembering how it was last used (`[StudioGuides]`). Snap, Lock, Duplicate and Delete are on a guide's right click (`KisAssistantToolMenu.cc`). A brush tool's bar shows a magnet while the image has guides. Captures don't show guides: test through what they change.
- **Picking layers** (`input/kis_select_layer_action`): every click picks, on release, the layer that shows most within about 3 screen px of the pointer (its alpha × opacity × what the layers above let through; within 1/255 the upper one wins), so a faint haze on top doesn't take every click and a thin line is easy to hit. What shows is what counts: filter layers neither count nor cover, darkening modes (Multiply, Darken, the burns) count by darkness, so white paper on them shows nothing, and a clipped layer counts only where its group has paint. Clicking again on the layer this last picked, within 6 screen px, or on the active one, picks the next in that order (state, not a timer); a drag past `dragDistance` moves the pressed-on layer. The hover pill (`KisLayerHover`) names the same layer, in the accent or, without one, the text color (`KisModernStyle::hasAccent`).
- **Export** (`KisStudioExport`, opened by Krita's export action while the Studio is on): PNG, JPEG and WebP as a busy part of the image shown at 2× as each would save it, each with the size of its file (the whole image encoded on a thread with Qt's writers, close to Krita's), Quality for the lossy ones, then only where. The settings go to Krita's filter with `skissaChosen`, which `KisImportExportManager::askUserAboutExportConfiguration` takes as already asked. Other formats… runs Krita's export. "Export again on each save" (for a .kra) links the image to that export (`StudioExportOnSave`: target, format, settings, and the target's time and size as the export left them); after each save it exports again, unless the file was changed by anything else since, which turns the link off with a message.
- **Make brush** (`KisStudioMakeBrush`): the selection's visible pixels become a picture tip (paper color made see-through, cropped, ≤1024 px; gray ones a mask, colored ones a stamp), saved in the brush panel's tag, picked, the quick editor opened on it.
- **Mixing** (`libs/pigment/compositeops/KoCompositeOpMix.h`, `KisStudioMixing`): a brush's paint mixes as Krita does (RGB), evenly (OKLab) or as pigment (a Mixbox-like model fitted in `C:\code\krita-deps\pigments-lab`), the brush setting `SkissaMixing`, chosen as three tiles in the quick editor's Color tab and in the brush sheet. Ops `skissa_mix_{oklab,pigment}_{over,copy}`, converting through the image's profile to linear (`KoCompositeOpInLinear`, keeping channel locks; 8-bit sRGB by tables, only the span a dab's mask covers: LCMS took two thirds of the time); the stroke uses them for Normal (`KisResourcesSnapshot`, smudging's `mixedCompositeOp`). Only the pigment over op is registered, as the layer blend mode Pigment (`KoMixing::layerMode`); brush blend lists leave it out (`KisCompositeOpComboBox::setForBrush`, `isLayerOnly`). Upstream Krita ignores the setting and reads the mode as Normal.
- **OKLCH blend modes** (`compositeops/KoCompositeOpOklch.h`): Hue, Chroma, Color and Lightness in OKLCH (`skissa_oklch_*`, `KoOklch::addTo`), out-of-gamut results giving up chroma. Registered with the mixing ops (`KoCompositeOpMix::all`). Upstream reads them as Normal.
- **Glow blends** (`compositeops/KoCompositeOpGlow.h`): Clip Studio's light modes, where the paint is scaled by its coverage and used as light, so soft, see-through paint glows fully. Add (glow) is Krita's own "Luminosity/Shine (SAI)" renamed in the registry (`KoGlow::addTo`; files stay Krita's); Glow dodge (`skissa_glow_dodge`, `cfGlowDodge`: under / (1 − paint × alpha), the paint as it is where nothing is under it) is added beside the SAI op in `KoCompositeOps.h`. Upstream reads Glow dodge as Normal. A picker's items added after users saved groups go once into their own category's group (`Source::addedLater`, remembered in `<groupsKey>Placed`, so taking one out again sticks).
- **Find** (`KisStudioFind`): one index of keyed items (`tool:…`, `effect:…`, `brush:…`, `image:…` recent images ranked by how recently opened, settings, commands with their classic menu path; layers by node, with the groups they are in; images and layers show their pictures, `PictureRole`), ranked by how it matches (exact, prefix, word start, initials, all words, inside, keywords, fuzzy), plus aliases from other apps; from three letters on the match decides and use only orders within it (typing "blur" puts Blur above a much-used Lens blur), with one or two letters use can pass a match. Uses fade with a time constant of 30 days (`Usage::countNow`, stored as the faded count at the last use), so what is used now outranks what was used often long ago. Right-click or long-press any button opens its card (`KisStudioItemCard`: shortcut as a re-recordable key cap, variants, pin/remove); a button with a card of its own sets `ownContextMenu`.
- **Curves** (`widgets/KisStudioCurve`): a tile's shape glides in (`glideNextChange`, the image updated once). Under Detail's and Noise's curves, dots from small to large say the axis is size (`paintSizes`); a strip of the layer blurred to each size was tried and read as noise.
- **Window frame** (`KisStudioWindowFrame`): the window draws its own title bar. Maximized it fills the work area asked from Windows (Qt's state lags), less 2 px on an auto-hiding taskbar's edge. Canvas-only from a maximized window stays maximized and covers the whole screen (`coverScreen`): Qt's full screen leaves through the restored size, which showed and laid everything out there for a moment. Window animations are off in canvas-only mode.
- **Its own folders** (`libs/global/KisProfile`): nothing is shared with a regular Krita. Config and logs in `<LocalAppData>/<name>/` (`KisProfile::configFile`), resources in `<AppData>/<name>/krita`, autosaves in `<Temp>/<name>` (the dev build `<name> dev`, its apart twin `<name> dev apart`, so neither offers or removes the other's live autosaves); its own single-instance key. Every new file goes through `KisProfile`, never a bare `QStandardPaths::GenericConfigLocation` or `"kritarc"`. The dev profile keeps the old layout in `qttest`; `KRITA_DEV_APART=1` runs the new layout there.
- **Name and links** (`KisProfile::name()`, `KisProfile::homepage()`): text meaning this app says Skissa; text about Krita the project, its files or docs stays Krita. Report Bug opens the homepage's issues. Krita's updater is off (`ENABLE_UPDATERS`).
- **Saving on Windows** (`KisSafeSave`, from `KisImportExportManager`): write beside, flush, swap with `ReplaceFileW` (keeps permissions, streams, cloud history), so a failed save never breaks the file; formats that write their own file (`supportsIO()` false) go through `KisSafeSave::besides` and `replaceWith`. A .kra's entries after the mimetype are Zip64 (`KoQuaZipStore`).
- **What Studio leaves out**: classic-only menu items are hidden while Studio is on (`hiddenToolbarActions`) and Find skips hidden actions. Panel plugins the Studio covers and the G'MIC bridge aren't built (`plugins/dockers/CMakeLists.txt`; sources stay for merges); the toolbox is only hidden (`KisStudioPanelPicker::isRetired`). After dropping a plugin, delete its DLL from the install: `cmake --install` never removes files.
## Dev workflow (Windows)

**Save tokens:** look at captures through `devtools\shot.ps1 -Out f.png -X -Y -W -H [-Scale 0.5]` (crop and shrink; never read full-size captures); read only the `docs/skissa/` file for the area at hand; grep with line numbers and read slices, not whole files; build with `dev.ps1 2>&1 | Select-Object -Last 3`.

The build environment is outside the repo, in `C:\code\krita-deps`: llvm-mingw, KDE's prebuilt Qt5 deps in `env\_install`, the build dir `build`, helper scripts in `devtools`, and in its root `run-krita.bat`, `profile.py`, `baseline-tests.bat` and `known-failing-tests.txt`.

- **The fork's own files in `libs/ui` are listed in `libs/ui/skissa.cmake`**, not kritaui's list (they rebuild in ~5 s instead of ~14 s). Add new `libs/ui` files there; form headers (`ui_*.h`) they include go in `kritaui_skissa_forms`.
- **One command:** `devtools\dev.ps1` builds, closes the dev Krita and waits, installs and launches (~10–20 s). Flags: `-NoBuild`, `-NoLaunch`. Build errors are matched case-sensitively; cmake's Python `ModuleNotFoundError` lines are harmless. After a build, check the installed DLL's time: an install can keep the old one if the dev Krita was still running. Two sessions building in `build` at once break CMake's regeneration: build again.
- **Always run the dev build through `run-krita.bat`** / `launchdev.ps1` (hidden console). It sets `KRITA_DEV_PROFILE=1`, so settings and resources live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\qttest` and `%APPDATA%\qttest`. **Never touch the owner's real Krita profile** (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\kritarc`, `%APPDATA%\krita`).
- **Driving the app:** the dev build listens for posted window messages (`krita/KisDevHooks.cpp`): `KritaDevCapture` (renders its own windows to a PNG; no screen capture, ever), `KritaDevMouse`, `KritaDevKey`, `KritaDevTree`, `KritaDevExport` (.kra; wParam 1 TIFF, 2 PNG, which asks for its options first; saved in the background as Export does, so the file lands a moment later, and not while the image stays busy), `KritaDevShortcuts`, `KritaDevColorDrop`, `KritaDevAction` (`devtools\action.ps1 -Name krita_filter_halftone` opens an effect; any action id runs it, the window's own too: `show_brush_editor`, `studio_effects`, `find_anything`, `options_configure`). Helpers in `devtools`: `devinput.ps1` (move, click, rclick, line, drag, type, key, capture), `pen.ps1` (a pressure stroke), `penrate.ps1` (`-Mouse` drags, `-Hover` moves, at a steady rate), `fxtime.ps1` (each effect's preview time, opened twice: the first opening carries one-off costs), `threads.ps1` (CPU per thread while a script block runs, named by start module), `colordrop.ps1`, `tree.ps1`, `kritawin.ps1`.
  - Captures miss QPainter-only canvas decorations (guides); GL-drawn tool outlines show.
  - Captures redraw every window, so stale pixels never show in them. `KritaDevCapture` with wParam 1 saves each window's backing store as it is instead (no GL canvas in it, see-through glass transparent): use it for anything "left behind".
  - Synthetic moves don't move the OS cursor: use the event's `globalPos`, not `QCursor::pos()`. They do hover item views (with `WA_Hover` on the viewport).
  - Synthetic presses go to the topmost window, a tooltip too: move and press without waiting. Capture before clicking by coordinates; the owner may have moved the bar.
  - Synthetic drags don't move the tip editor's chips or Grow/Soft scrubbers: test those through what they change.
  - In popups, `QContextMenuEvent::pos()` can be in window coordinates: map from `globalPos()`.
  - In a fresh profile (`KRITA_DEV_APART`) the UI scale differs, so devinput coordinates are logical pixels.
- **The shell sees a stale copy of `%LOCALAPPDATA%`** (the desktop app is an MSIX package). To read or edit the real dev `kritarc`, copy it with a WMI-launched process (`Invoke-CimMethod Win32_Process Create`), and edit it only while the dev Krita is closed. Console programs started through WMI hang when Windows Terminal is the default console: start them detached (`CreateFlags = 8`, `ShowWindow = 0`).
- **Testing beside the owner:** start the apart twin (`KRITA_DEV_APART=1` through `run-krita.bat`) on a copy of an image in the scratchpad, never with no file: it reopens its last image, and two instances on one file overwrite each other's autosave. Set `KRITA_DEV_PID` to its process id: every helper in `devtools` that picks the dev Krita honours it (without it they take the first one found, which may be the owner's). Its profile is its own, so the owner's needs no backup. A rebuild still closes both.
- **Back up the dev profile right before each test that writes to it,** and restore exactly what was there; the owner works in the dev build between turns. Prefer the UI (with the panel's Undo) over editing files. Ctrl+Z in a test also undoes settings toggles.
- **It's fine to close the dev Krita** (unsaved changes and all) to rebuild. Only kill processes whose path is under `C:\code\krita-deps` (the owner's installed Krita may be running; scripts look for `skissa` and `krita`). After a kill, delete `.sunset.png-autosave.kra`, or the next start waits on recovery.
- Another session may be driving the dev Krita (maybe with other variables): a fresh-looking profile in a capture may be that, not the code.
- **README clips:** `devtools\rec.py` drives the app and captures frames (stop-motion, ~1 s a capture); save as animated WebP.
- **Pen-to-pixel time:** `devtools\latency.ps1` (with the dev build closed; `-Running` measures one started that way) paints scripted strokes at a pen's report rate (`penrate.ps1`) with `KRITA_DEV_LATENCY` and prints each stage's median/95th/worst ms (`KisPenLatency`: arrived, dabs handed to the engine, merged into the image, taken by the canvas, frame drawn). At 200 Hz: about 11 ms median, 13 at the 95th; 5 of it is the wait for the next report (each curve piece is painted once the report after it gives its tangent), every other stage under 2 ms. A pen that stops before lifting gets its last piece after three of its reports (20–100 ms, `reportSpacing` in the freehand helper; Krita waited 100 ms). Check the canvas is OpenGL (`KRITA_DEV_FRAMELOG` names `KisOpenGLCanvas2`): the spare profile has had OpenGL fail and measured the QPainter canvas.
- **Startup profiling:** `KRITA_DEV_SAMPLE=<file>`, then `profile.py <file> --timeline 250` (`--callers`/`--callees`). `QT_LOGGING_RULES=krita.performance.debug=true` prints Krita's phase timings. Most start time is Krita's. `KRITA_DEV_FRAMELOG` gives each frame's cost and paints.
- **UI tour:** `KRITA_DEV_TOUR=<folder>` runs `krita/KisDevTour.cpp`, photographing the whole UI (the crash sweep); `devtools\tour_index.py` and `tour_maps.py` index it. Run it with `KRITA_DEV_APART=1` after closing the dev Krita (a second one hands itself over), and not while tests build. It picks what to press by text, tooltip, object or accessible name: unnamed buttons may get Close pressed. Every early return in a step must `end()` it. Its photos have artifacts (popup order, pointer-placed pickers, transparent glass dialogs): check findings by hand.
- **Tests:** `devtools\tests.ps1` builds unit tests in `build-tests`, runs them, reruns failures one at a time, and reports failures untouched Krita doesn't share (`known-failing-tests.txt`, checked against `build-upstream` with `baseline-tests.bat`). Run after merges from Krita and changes to Krita's code (libs/image, pigment, flake, formats). Tests load plugins from the install: `dev.ps1 -NoLaunch` first when testing a plugin. A timeout of `TestCompositeOpInversion` on a busy machine isn't a failure (`--timeout 900`). `kis_processings_test`'s two transform tests fail by design: their Blur filter layer follows the scale (Effects follow transforms), Krita's reference images don't.
- **Test images:** `C:\code\krita-deps\sunset.png` (the `launchdev.ps1` default); `glass-test.jpg` is the Krita artwork.

## Updating from Krita

`origin` is Krita (KDE), `skissa` is the fork's own repo. Merge Krita in often (small merges are easy); never rebase the branch.

- **One step:** `devtools\update.ps1` runs `packaging/skissa-update.py`: fetch, merge (rerere on), translations refreshed and committed, build, install, start and capture. Exit 2 leaves the merge open: settle, commit, run it again. Check the capture, then push.
- **Check a merge first:** `git merge-tree --write-tree --name-only HEAD origin/master` lists the files that would conflict.
- **Keep Krita's files mergeable:** new code in new files called with a line or two; turn Krita's code off in CMake rather than deleting it; don't append to Krita's lists when a fork file can hold it (`po-skissa/`, `libs/ui/skissa.cmake`).
- **Skissa's own copies of Krita's files**, built in their place: the layer, palette and time-lapse panels (`plugins/dockers/skissa_layers`, `skissa_palette`, `skissa_recorder`) and Multibrush (`KisStudioMultiBrush`). Krita's fixes to those don't merge: the update script lists Krita's commits that touched them (`KEPT` in `skissa-update.py`) to bring over by hand.
- **Where the fork still changes Krita's code most** (candidates to move behind a call): `KisMainWindow.cpp`, `kis_tool_freehand_helper.cpp`, `plugins/paintops/libpaintop` option widgets, `kis_preset_chooser.cpp`, `kis_paintop_settings_widget.cpp`, `KisOpenGLCanvasRenderer.cpp`, `kis_config.cc`, `kis_slider_spin_box_p.h`, `kis_cmb_composite.cc`, `kis_paintop_box.cc`, `KisTagChooserWidget.cpp`. ## Legal and credit

- Never use Krita's name or logo as this app's name or icon. "An independent fork of Krita" in plain text is fine, with the not-affiliated line (README, NOTICE, About dialog).
- Keep every copyright and SPDX header; new files get the owner's header (`GeorgeAzma`). GPL-3.0 section 5a: the modification notice stays in README, NOTICE and the About dialog.
- Credit art by author, title and license: the Kiki splash is Tyson Tan's, CC BY-SA 4.0. Only use images whose source is known.
- A release zip ships the third-party license texts and points to the dependencies' sources; each release links its exact git tag.
- The pigment mixing constants are fitted to Mixbox (non-commercial); a clean refit in the lab awaits the owner's OK.

## Code style

- Match Krita's style: 4 spaces, the file's brace style, `i18nc` with a context for every user-visible string.
- **Comments are short, natural English** that say why or what is not obvious. No history ("was", "now", "fixed"), no mention of reviews or conversations, no comments that restate the code.
- No dead code, no commented-out code, no debug output left behind.
- **Commit messages:** a subject that says what the change does for the user ("Brush settings: each row is its value"), a short body if needed, and the `Co-Authored-By` line the session asks for. Stage only your own files and hunks: other agents may be working in the same tree.

## Open questions and known issues

- **The app is named Skissa** everywhere it names itself. The program is `skissa.exe` (`skissa.com` in a console; `krita/skissa.cmake`), and a tiny `krita.exe` beside it only starts it (`krita/skissa_bridge.cpp`) for old pins and pre-rename updates. Internal ids stay (`krita` application name, `.kra`): `main.cc` sets the application name before anything reads it. The splash art is Kiki (credited); art of the fork's own would separate it further.
- **The homepage is `github.com/GeorgeAzma/skissa`** (`KisProfile::homepage()`, `make-release.py`); change both if the repo moves.
- **The README says plainly that all the code is AI-written** and the ideas are the owner's; keep that true and visible. Its donate button goes to Krita.
- **Pre-5.0 brush engine paths were removed** (legacy smudge, spray center bias, hard flow). Old presets that used them paint slightly differently.
- **G'MIC-Qt isn't built**, nor the bridge. The idea on the table is wrapping the useful G'MIC filters as native effects.
- **The owner asked to be reminded** of shader filters and custom blend modes written as shader-like code (Shadertoy-style GLSL in the effect panel, sliders from uniforms); they liked the idea.
- **Performance left on the table:** Krita's compressor delays on costly slots are one frame (`KisFrameRate::frameMs()`); a floor such as 30 ms for the costly ones would save work. In Krita's code: `KisFilterManager::apply` waits on the GUI thread, `KisUpdateOutlineJob` redoes the whole outline, `KisToolUtils::findNodes` reads every layer, `KisPaintopBox::slotUpdateOptionsWidgetPopup` re-reads the whole editor. The fork's own: long stabilized replays build per-millisecond samples, Find scores every entry per key and rebuilds its index at every opening (much of it has no change signal), a pinned brush setting makes the paintop box re-read the whole editor on each outside change, the line-art grow reads the fill's whole bounding box (a band around the fill changes results), a color drop computes its fill again after the preview (antialiasing and the selection differ).
- **A layer dragged off the panel onto the canvas is deleted** (undoable); an overshoot while reordering does it too. Keep, or delete only when dropped outside the window?
- **Pen pressure:** a tablet driver can refuse WinTab to every program (seen with an XP-Pen, cause unknown). Skissa defaults to Windows Ink (`useWin8PointerInput`) and falls back to it for the session when WinTab is refused (`KisWinTab::refused()`, `krita/KisWinTab.cpp`). Test the app side with `pen.ps1`; for a real pen, log whether the canvas gets tablet or mouse presses.
- **Left from the November 2026 review:** Light's and Radial blur's light isn't reclipped (a white plateau near white lamps; a look the owner tuned); Detail, Blur and Smooth average in the image's encoding, not linear light; the picker can only make sRGB colors; Krita's Feather (and so the Fill tool's) is a gaussian of sigma N cut at one sigma, unlike Soft (changing it changes Krita's selection tests); a Find of a group or vector layer's effect does nothing silently (the picker says why); an autosave declined in the start-up recovery dialog can still be deleted where there is no Recycle Bin (`KisApplication::checkAutosaveFiles`). Not undoable though arranged by hand: rings, the Controls panel, picker groups, the color picker's style, symmetry's settings.
- **Ideas weighed and left:** handles to nudge a snapped shape, time-lapse trimming, a theme taken from the image, a canvas flip gesture, lasso fill (Krita's Freehand Path brush does it).
